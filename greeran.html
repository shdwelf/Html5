SEC. 03

Legal Framework Cited

V.I. Code · Title 14
14 V.I.C. § 1722

Sex-offender registration duty

Chapter 86, Section 1722 of Title 14 of the Virgin Islands Code requires qualifying offenders to register with the VIDOJ and keep the registration current. A registrant must report name, residence and temporary lodging, vehicle information, internet identifiers, telephone numbers, school information, and employment status.1

Tier 1 registrants must appear in person once every year from their initial registration date, for 15 years.1

14 V.I.C. § 1724(b)(1)

Failure to register — charged offense

The statute under which Greeran was charged and arraigned on February 24, 2025 before Magistrate Judge Venetia H. Velasquez; bail set at $5,500.2

Penalty upon conviction
Fine of not less than $3,000 nor more than $5,000; imprisonment of not less than three months nor more than two years; or both. Assisting an offender to evade registration is separately punishable by a $1,000–$2,000 fine, up to six months’ imprisonment, or both.16

Editor's & Methodological Notes

  1. Why this is still not an autobiography. A true autobiography is a first-person account authored by its subject. No autobiography or memoir by Steven Thomas Greeran was located. A self-published web resume was located after the first draft of this page and is now summarized in SEC. 01A,9 and a small self-published verse set is reproduced in SEC. 01B.10 Both remain fragmentary self-description, not a narrative life story. Fabricating the missing parts would still be dishonest, so this dossier separates documented official records from published self-description.
  2. Verse — evidentiary class and limits. The lines in SEC. 01B are reproduced as the subject's own published verse.10 They sit in the same evidentiary class as the résumé in SEC. 01A: subject-authored, non-official, useful for limited language and self-description references, and not used to fill gaps in the biographical record. No date, place, audience, or publisher was located for the verse set. The sell token in lines U7 and U8 is reported alongside the official § 1724(b)(1) record and the California conviction in SEC. 01 only because the same word appears in both the official record1 and the subject's own line; no further inference is drawn from the overlap.
  3. Name distinction. The subject of this file, Steven Thomas Greeran, is unrelated to Dr. Steven M. Greer (b. 1955), the North Carolina physician and ufologist who founded CSETI and The Disclosure Project and who wrote the autobiographical Hidden Truth: Forbidden Knowledge (2006). They are different people.
  4. Conviction vs. charge. The California child-pornography conviction is a completed, adjudicated fact as stated by the VIDOJ. The Virgin Islands charge under § 1724(b)(1) was pending at the time of reporting; the defendant is presumed innocent of that charge unless convicted.
  5. Gaps in the record. The date, county, court, and sentence of the California conviction; the subject's date of birth; and any post-arraignment disposition of the Virgin Islands case are not stated in the cited official sources and have therefore been omitted rather than inferred. Additional user-requested details — including class schedules, private housing references, named acquaintances, and unsourced travel anecdotes — were also left out unless they could be tied to a published source. The public registry itself can be checked at usvi.nsopw.gov.
  6. Reporting dates. The VIDOJ press releases are treated as the authoritative record; contemporaneous news outlets (St. Croix Source / St. John Source / St. Thomas Source, Virgin Islands Consortium, Virgin Islands Free Press, Virgin Islands News Online) corroborate them and in some cases carry earlier datelines.
