# Isolated Line Test Set — bill of materials

Generated by `tools/build-wallplug-eagle.mjs` from `tools/xport-v92-linetestset-model.mjs`.
Rev A, 110 × 80 mm, 2 layers, 1.57 mm FR-4.

**DNP** = designed-in but not fitted by default.

Two parts are not interchangeable. T1 must be a "wet" 600:600 ohm telecom transformer, specified to carry 20-50 mA of DC loop current without saturating - a dry voice transformer of the same impedance works on the bench and fails the moment the line goes off-hook. BR1 must be rated for the line: 600 V reverse, since ring and surge both sit above anything a signal diode survives. Everything else is generic through-hole stock.

## Safety

* The left of the board is line-referenced. 48 V DC sits on the pair, ring peaks above 150 V, and a lightning or power-cross event puts far more on it.
* T1 is the only isolation barrier between the line and the earpiece you put against your head. Do not substitute a transformer that is not rated for the line.
* Isolation resistance from the line side to any touchable metal has to hold 5 Mohm measured at 100 V DC. Measure it after assembly, before first use.
* The 3 mm creepage across the barrier is enforced by the design-rule checker and backed by a keep-out slot. Do not bridge it with a jumper or a solder blob.
* Use only on lines you are authorised to work on. Connecting to a line that is not yours, or listening to a call you are not a party to, is illegal in most jurisdictions regardless of what the hardware is capable of.
