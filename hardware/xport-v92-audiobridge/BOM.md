# POTS Audio Bridge — bill of materials

Generated by `tools/build-wallplug-eagle.mjs` from `tools/xport-v92-audiobridge-model.mjs`.
Rev A, 120 × 90 mm, 2 layers, 1.57 mm FR-4.

**DNP** = designed-in but not fitted by default.

The one part that is not interchangeable is T1. It has to be a "wet" 600:600 ohm telecom transformer: specified to carry the 20-50 mA of DC loop current without saturating. A dry voice transformer of the same impedance will work on the bench and go quiet or distort the moment the line goes off-hook. Everything else is generic through-hole stock.

## Safety

* The left of the board is line-referenced. 48 V DC sits on the pair, ring peaks above 150 V, and a lightning or power-cross event puts far more on it. Do not touch that end while it is connected.
* T1 is the only isolation barrier. If you replace it with anything other than a transformer rated for the line, the sound card and the PC chassis become part of the telephone circuit.
* This design is not a certified Direct Access Arrangement and is not approved for connection to a public network. Put an approved DAA in front of it, or keep it behind a PBX or on a bench line.
* Isolation resistance from the line side to any touchable metal has to hold 5 Mohm measured at 100 V DC. Measure it after assembly, not before.
* The 3 mm creepage between the line side and the SELV side is enforced by the design-rule checker and backed by a milled slot. Do not bridge the slot with a jumper or a solder blob.
