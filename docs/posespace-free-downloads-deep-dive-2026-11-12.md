# PoseSpace free downloads: a research deep dive

*Free browsing, sample downloads, book bonuses, educational permissions, and provenance — researched 2026-11-12 UTC.*

## 0. Scope and honesty note

This continues the repository's source-checking approach in the [Fravia research note](fravia-searchlores-deep-dive-2026-11-01.md) and the [PDFDrive provenance note](pdfdrive-deep-dive-2026-10-13.md). Search results are leads, not proof of current availability or licensing.

- **Verified (live)** means a page or metadata record was fetched and its text or links observed on the research date. A site's statements remain its own claims.
- **Indexed** means a search snippet reported a claim; it may be stale or incomplete.
- **Historical / third-party** identifies older accounts or sources independent of PoseSpace.
- **Unverified** identifies a workflow or detail not actually tested.

No account was created, no email confirmed, no purchase made, no book code redeemed, and no archive downloaded or opened. The extractor returned links and page text, but cannot establish that a ZIP successfully downloads or inspect its contents. Counts, prices, and policies may change. PoseSpace warns that its site includes artistic nudity and is for adults; the public sample described below is clothed.

## 1. Findings at a glance

1. **Browse freely, but do not confuse previews with free full-resolution files.** PoseSpace's [homepage](https://posespace.com/) says its 7,000+ poses can be browsed free; the [Pose Tool](https://posespace.com/posetool/) says sample photos are 512 pixels and purchases can be as high as 48 megapixels. Another [official explanation](https://posespace.com/howdoesitwork.aspx) calls previews watermarked and 1 megapixel, and quotes up to 50 MP for purchases. These are conflicting descriptions, not independently measured sizes.
2. **One readily accessible official sample is advertised without a form.** The [Zaza016 sample page](https://posespace.com/sample.aspx) links 24 individual images labelled full-size and advertises a large complete-pose ZIP. The related [Art Models gateway](https://www.artmodels.us/) explicitly links a ZIP for the same pose. Neither the image dimensions nor the ZIP download or contents were tested.
3. **Two more full-size poses are advertised with a free login and email confirmation.** The [sample page](https://posespace.com/sample.aspx) states the confirmation requirement; [Pose Reference](https://posespace.com/pose-reference/) depicts Jenni026 and Jesse003 and states two samples, 48 photos each. Actual new-account entitlements, names, files, and counts were not checked.
4. **Book-owner bonuses and educational allowances are separate routes**, with conditions. [Art Models Bonus](https://posespace.com/free/) asks book owners to have an ISBN or a code; [educational terms](https://posespace.com/license.aspx) describe limited photo distribution, live display, video previews, and discounts.
5. **“Royalty-free” does not mean public domain.** The [FAQ](https://posespace.com/sitenotes.aspx) permits reference use for traditional art and says the resulting work may be sold; it prohibits redistribution and collage use of the actual photos, subject to separate permissions and educational terms.
6. **Some free-pose claims are historical, not a current checkout test.** A 2020 [news post](https://posespace.com/news.aspx) said ShawneC poses were free via the credits button; a logged-out [current model listing](https://posespace.com/posetool/?mn=shawnec) showed 12 poses at $4.99 and 4 credits each. We did not test authenticated redemption.

## 3. The book bonus: separate from the public sample

The live [bonus page](https://posespace.com/free/) describes the offer as a thank-you for **owning Art Models**, not an unrestricted collection of free poses. The [ISBN-parameter landing page](https://posespace.com/free/landing.aspx?isbn=9781947728134) shows retailer review links and a link back to PoseSpace titled “Get Your Bonus Pose.” It does **not** establish the full redemption sequence.

A [libresearch query](https://libresearch.space/search?q=%22posespace.com%2Ffree%2F%22+code+ISBN+get+these+2+poses+free) indexed retailer and other book excerpts describing a code and the `/free/` page. Such snippets are historical leads, not proof that any publicly visible ISBN qualifies a visitor or still works. A later direct fetch of the [BenP028 retailer page](https://www.barnesandnoble.com/w/art-models-benp028-douglas-johnson/1126271388) confirmed the product listing, not the indexed code text. No code was entered. Use a legitimately owned book's instructions and ask the publisher if the process is unclear.

## 4. How paid access differs

[How PoseSpace works](https://posespace.com/howdoesitwork.aspx) describes a Pose as a ZIP of JPEGs, usually a 360-degree set; [the FAQ](https://posespace.com/sitenotes.aspx) notes exceptions with selected views or variations. A complete turnaround commonly has 24 views at 15-degree intervals, but this is not universal.

| Product | Site description, as observed |
| --- | --- |
| Individual Pose | Typically $6.99 or 5 credits according to the [Pose Tool](https://posespace.com/posetool/); prices vary. Purchased Poses are added to the user's Library for repeat downloads, according to [How it works](https://posespace.com/howdoesitwork.aspx). |
| Pose Credits | The [credits page](https://posespace.com/shop/credits.aspx) lists 10 credits for $12 and says credits are non-refundable. It describes using the `!` button rather than the cart. Prices and credit requirements can change. |
| Sessions and Companion Downloads | Separate larger downloads rather than individual Library Poses. [How it works](https://posespace.com/howdoesitwork.aspx) says they eventually expire and need no account; [getDownload](https://posespace.com/shop/getDownload.aspx) asks for a confirmation code or transaction/invoice ID. These are compatible claims: no persistent account does not mean no purchase code. |
| Example companion disk | The [Art Models 2 page](https://posespace.com/shop/DLCompDisk.aspx) displayed $29.99, 632 MB and a limit of five downloads in seven days. It inconsistently describes 64 poses in product text and 61 in its list heading; verify the product before buying. |

A broad anonymous Pose Tool request returned a count of 7,504 and examples as low as $1.49, but the supplied sort parameter did **not** reliably establish an ascending ordering (prices appeared out of order). It cannot establish the catalogue's minimum price or prove no zero-credit poses exist.

## 5. Licensing and educational use

The [FAQ](https://posespace.com/sitenotes.aspx) says traditional drawings, paintings and sculptures made using photos as reference can be sold; it distinguishes those works from a collage using the photographs. It also describes permitted games, videos and digital art after substantive manipulation, giving a wireframe mesh, skin or texture as examples. The FAQ says photographs may not be resold, repackaged for sale, or given away in whole or part. The [sample page](https://posespace.com/sample.aspx) expressly says royalty-free does **not** mean copyright-free. [Educational use](https://posespace.com/license.aspx) says all photos are copyright Douglas Johnson.

The educational page describes **specific exceptions and conditions**:

- Display any number of photos for a live, non-recorded class, with students told the images are supplied by and used with permission of PoseSpace.
- Give students up to **five full-resolution photos, not five full Poses**, after notifying the publisher by email and displaying the specified permission notice.
- Use its `/img/512/{Pose Name}.jpg` preview photos in instructional videos and email the publisher to say where they are used.
- Request details of the HD-resolution Educator's Pack; teachers can ask about a 50% discount for licensed copies for students.

These are summaries, not a substitute for current terms or legal advice. For questions about redistribution, recorded courses, products, ambiguous digital transformations, or uses not expressly covered, [contact PoseSpace](https://posespace.com/m/cf.aspx). Commercial artwork rights do not automatically grant permission to redistribute source photographs.

The site's [robots.txt](https://posespace.com/robots.txt) declares `search=yes,ai-train=no,use=reference` and says content-signal restrictions reserve rights under EU Directive 2019/790 Article 4. It also lists crawler-specific rules. This is the site's stated machine-readable policy, **not a determination of rights in every jurisdiction**; it should not be treated as permission to build a training dataset.

## 6. Historical free poses: a useful contradiction

A targeted search on an instance selected from [searx.space](https://searx.space/) surfaced an older first-party announcement. In its [2020-11-15 news entry](https://posespace.com/news.aspx), PoseSpace wrote that ShawneC poses were free in the Pose Tool and instructed users to use `!` (buy with credits), warning that **Add to Cart would charge**.

The [live ShawneC query](https://posespace.com/posetool/?mn=shawnec), accessed logged out on 2026-11-12, displayed 12 items at **$4.99 / 4 credits each**. The 2020 announcement is therefore not sufficient evidence of a current free offer. It is possible that promotions or account-dependent pricing changed; we did not log in or click to confirm either explanation. Do not approve a paid transaction on the strength of old news copy.

A [2008 WetCanvas thread](https://www.wetcanvas.com/forums/topic/free-download-reference-photo-or-a-virtual-life-class/) reported a different promotion for Becca029 at zero credits, but the [current logged-out lookup](https://posespace.com/posetool/?p=becca029) displays $6.99 / 5 credits. The current educational page still says several other free poses can be found in the Pose Tool, but gives no list. **Current additional zero-credit entitlement remains unresolved.**

## 8. Archive provenance warning

The [Internet Archive item metadata](https://archive.org/metadata/posespace-archive) for `posespace-archive` labels the item Public Domain Mark 1.0 and attributes it to “Livemodelsbooks.” It lists a ZIP and a cover image; the notes describe three PDFs, but the visible manifest does not establish the ZIP's actual contents. The item's displayed publication and added dates differ; metadata alone does not resolve when the underlying works were created or whether their uploader was authorized.

That public-domain label conflicts with PoseSpace's [copyright and educational page](https://posespace.com/license.aspx) and its [FAQ's](https://posespace.com/sitenotes.aspx) restrictions on giving photos away. The [Public Domain Mark](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/) is an identification of a work as free of known copyright restrictions, not proof that the identification is correct or that other rights are absent. The metadata alone does not establish uploader authority, and the files were not inspected. **Do not treat this record as a verified authorized download**; contact the rights holder if permission matters. The item's metadata says it was checked for malware, but that neither proves rights nor verifies the contents' safety now. This note links to metadata for provenance, not to the bundle.

## 10. Open questions and next research steps

1. With the user's own account and consent, verify which two Poses appear after email confirmation, file sizes and photo counts, and whether other current zero-credit poses exist; record the price shown **before** confirming any purchase.
2. Test the public sample's ZIP in an ordinary browser, measure the files and compare it with the ArtModels.us link if a byte-level comparison is useful. Do not assume the extractable page link proves a successful ZIP.
3. With a legitimately owned book, follow the bonus instructions and record what the user actually receives and any expiry or account requirements.
4. Ask PoseSpace to clarify the 512-pixel versus 1-megapixel preview statements, how to locate currently free Poses, current educational permissions and the archive item's authorization.
5. Re-run the logged metasearch queries on other healthy searx.space instances and record dates, engines and negative results; cached snippets and rankings can change.
6. Review each alternative's current commercial-art and source-photo redistribution terms independently.
