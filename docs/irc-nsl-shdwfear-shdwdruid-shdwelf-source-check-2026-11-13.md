# Public IRC source check: Null Space Labs `#nsl`

**Review date: 2026-11-13.** This follow-up checks the literal handles `shdwfear`, `shdwdruid`, and `shdwelf` against accessible historical IRC sources and indexed secondary leads. It follows the [EFnet `#pub` source map](historical-networks-and-source-leads-2026-11-13.md) and the separate [`#2600`/`enocia` check](irc-2600-shdwfear-enocia-source-check-2026-11-13.md). It is **not** a transcript, a search of every IRC server's private client logs, or an attempt to identify the people behind nicknames.

**Result: no verified message by any of the three handles in EFnet `#nsl` was recovered.** Null Space Labs identifies its channel as **EFnet `#nsl`** ([current wiki](https://wiki.032.la/), [older NSL page](https://wiki.032.la/doku.php?id=null_space_labs)); each links to `irc://irc.efnet.org/nsl`. That establishes the intended **network and channel**, not the origin of a particular indexed snippet. Other networks can have a `#nsl` with the same spelling; none may be conflated with this one.

A separate [CTFtime date cross-reference](ctftime-ucsb-shellphish-null-space-labs-ictf-date-cross-reference-2026-11-13.md) lists **Null Space Labs' CTF-team scoreboard results** and compares UCSB/Shellphish iCTF event dates with the 2006 `#ictf` log. CTF participation records are not IRC messages and do not authenticate this channel or any of its nicks.

## Exact-handle outcome

- **`shdwfear`:** Search-indexed [Reddit `r/NSL` item A](https://www.reddit.com/r/NSL/comments/1njjcb/httplinksmkt1985comcttkn89msnjgznzcyoas2rmjc2mtg4n/) and [item B](https://www.reddit.com/r/NSL/comments/1njze9/httpwwwgooglecomurlqhttp3a2f2fwwwgatestoneinstitut/) display chat-*formatted* nick/time fragments; other [Reddit domain-index results](https://www.reddit.com/domain/isis.poly.edu/) show posts attributed to `NSLbot` with nick-formatted link text. These are **secondary indexed snippets/bot repost leads only**. Direct reads of the two posts returned **HTTP 403**; no underlying IRC log, original network/channel, complete date with timezone, or surrounding message context was verified. A clock time alone is not a date. We did not follow the outgoing links embedded in those posts or quote their text as an EFnet `#nsl` line.
- **`shdwdruid`:** No verified `#nsl` line or indexed target-channel log found in the checked sources. This is not proof that the handle was absent from IRC.
- **`shdwelf`:** No verified `#nsl` line or indexed target-channel log found in the checked sources. A similar spelling in another platform or repository would not establish use of this IRC nick.

A bot repost, search preview, NAMES/JOIN/QUIT event, and contemporaneous IRC `PRIVMSG` are different evidence types. [IRC Help's EFnet FAQ](https://www.irchelp.org/ircd/efnetfaq.html#q2) explains that EFnet nicknames and channels are not registered identities. Do not promote the Reddit leads into a conversation, link the three nicks to real people, or treat any matching nick across networks as the same operator.

## What would permit a more decisive check

A dated, rights-cleared **EFnet `#nsl`** channel log from an original client/bot/operator, an independently readable archival URL, or a bounded year range could be checked for each exact handle (and for case variants). Record source owner and capture method, hash the original bytes, retain neighboring lines, resolve timezone, and confirm that the session really used EFnet and `#nsl` before quoting a message. A live connection today cannot retrieve years of past channel messages. Keep any private raw logs and genuinely recovered private conversation in owner-controlled Drive, **not this public repository**; publish only provenance and a non-sensitive finding after review. No such private chat material was recovered in this pass, so there is nothing to upload.
