## Cross-reference: does this contract still exist?

See [`jcreator-jdk-coupling.md`](jcreator-jdk-coupling.md) for the OpenJDK side, verified directly against `openjdk/jdk`, `openjdk/jdk8u`, `openjdk/jdk17u` and `openjdk/jdk21u`. In short: the JDK launcher's own registry lookup lived in `java_md.c` through JDK 8, was compiled out behind a never-defined `USE_REGISTRY_LOOKUP` by JDK 17, and the last vestige was deleted in 2024. The `Java Development Kit` key that a third-party IDE needed appears nowhere in the OpenJDK tree at all.
