## 2. Keyhole, Inc. Founding Team & Corporate Genesis

- **Origins**: Spun off in 2001 from **Intrinsic Graphics** (a 3D graphics gaming software firm founded by former SGI engineers).
- **Core Founders (GSA 2010 President's Medal Citation)**:
  - **John Hanke** — CEO & Co-founder (former US Foreign Service; later VP Google Geo, founder Niantic / Pokémon GO).
  - **Chikai Ohazama** — Co-founder & Chief Software Architect (spatial data processing pipeline).
  - **Mark Aubin** — Co-founder & Lead Software Engineer (3D rendering and client engine).
  - **Phil Keslin** — Co-founder & Chief System Architect (spatial streaming protocols; later CTO of Niantic).
  - **Avi Bar-Zeev** — Co-founder & 3D interaction architect (earlier VRML pioneer, later Microsoft HoloLens / Apple).
- **Advisory Founders & Technical Leadership**:
  - **Brian McClendon** — Co-founder, angel investor, Board Member, and VP of Engineering (later VP Google Geo).
  - **Michael T. Jones** — Chief Technologist (later Chief Technology Advocate at Google).
  - **Chris Tanner** & **Rémi Arnaud** — Intrinsic Graphics graphics engine architects.

## 3. Investor Mapping: Angels, Strategic Corporates & Institutional VC

Keyhole, Inc. raised approximately **$4.6M** across multiple rounds and strategic bundling arrangements between 2001 and 2004 before being acquired by Google:

### 3.1 Angel Investors
- **Brian McClendon**: Provided initial personal angel capital during the spin-off from Intrinsic Graphics in 2001, prior to joining Keyhole full-time as VP of Engineering and board member.
- **George T. Harber**: Early private angel investor participating alongside initial management.

### 3.2 Strategic Corporate Investors & Bundling Deals
- **Sony Corporation (Sony Venture Capital)**: First-round corporate venture investment, interested in 3D visualization and PlayStation-era graphics applications.
- **NVIDIA Corporation**: Strategic seed/equity investment and bundling arrangement (April 18, 2002); packaged EarthViewer trials with GeForce graphics cards to showcase desktop 3D hardware capabilities.

### 3.3 Intelligence Community & Government: In-Q-Tel (IQT) / NIMA
- **In-Q-Tel Investment**: June 25, 2003 (engagement began February 2003).
- **Agency Sponsoring Agency**: **National Imagery and Mapping Agency (NIMA)**, renamed in November 2003 to the **National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency (NGA)**.
- **Context & Operational Use**: NIMA utilized Keyhole's streaming 3D geospatial engine during Operation Iraqi Freedom (2003), where news networks (e.g., CNN, CBS) and military commanders used EarthViewer live on-air and in briefing rooms to visualize Baghdad topography.

### 3.4 Institutional VC Term Sheets & The "Series G" Exit
- In 2004, facing intense server demands and seeking expansion capital, Keyhole secured term sheets for a **$10 million Series B round** at a **$30 million valuation**, led by **Menlo Ventures**.
- Additional interest came from GIS titan **Esri (Jack Dangermond)**.
- Before closing the Series B, **Google** (championed by Sergey Brin) intervened with an outright acquisition offer in October 2004 (valued at ~$35 million in Google stock), affectionately dubbed the "Series G" exit by the founders.
