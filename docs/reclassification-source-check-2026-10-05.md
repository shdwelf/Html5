## 2. Discrepancies and open items

1. **Order date Apr. 22 vs. Apr. 23, 2026** — Jackson Lewis says the order is *dated* Apr. 22 and *announced* Apr. 23; most sources use Apr. 23. Package uses "Apr. 22–23."
2. **Canna Provisions cert status** — petition filed Oct. 2025, pending as of Dec. 2025 sources; whether cert was denied in 2026 was not confirmed today. Flagged in both files that cite it.
3. **Scope nuance in secondary sources** — some trade coverage says the April order moved "all FDA-approved marijuana products and state-licensed medical products"; CRS says Δ9-THC-derived products within the CSA marijuana definition. CRS wording adopted.
4. **GAO report number** — title and date verified; the GAO accession number (GAO-26-xxxxx) was not captured. Obtain from gao.gov before formal citation.
5. **Illustrative figures** — every dollar amount in the budget file is bracketed [ILLUSTRATIVE]; JCT/CBO scores for 280E effects were not sourced this session and are deliberately left blank rather than guessed.
6. **Statutory quotations** — §§ 812(b), 841(b), 844(a), 811(d)(1) quoted from session knowledge of the U.S. Code; wording is believed accurate but must be checked against uscode.house.gov before use in filings.
