## GOLD vs RBT

- **GOLD — Gaucho On-Line Data**, gnet.ucsb.edu/gold (Wayback 20010816000708,
  `gold.htm`, Aug 2001): address updates, BARC billing, financial aid, academic
  history, registration info/appointments, personal info, class schedule + add/drop,
  degree summary, official transcript ordering (new), registration sticker status,
  Schedule of Classes search + registration, student directory, registrar calendar.

  systems coexisted in print; GOLD was the web path a 2001 freshman used.

## Dining, the song, and the coast

- **DLG (De La Guerra)** dining commons by the UCen.
- **Jack Johnson, "Bubble Toes"** (*Brushfire Fairytales*, Feb 2001; Johnson is a
  UCSB CCS film grad): "Well I was eating lunch at the D. L. G." + "her feet are all
  covered with tar balls and scars."
- **Coal Oil Point seep field**: ~3 km² offshore Goleta, Offshore South Ellwood
  field; ~100–150 bbl oil/day, ~40 t methane/day; tar balls on Goleta/IV beaches;
  seepage near **Platform Holly** dropped ~50% 1973–95 (Quigley et al. 1999,
  *Geology* 27(11):1047); UCSB beach monitoring **2001–03** peaked ~930,000
  tarballs/km at Coal Oil Point Beach.

## The campus map and the monarchs

- Lagoon (restored estuary), Campus Point, Goleta slough, art studio, Long Range
  Development Plan (campus master plan).
- **Monarchs**: Ellwood Mesa grove (Goleta Butterfly Grove); arrive mid-October,
  peak mid-December, gone by mid-March; documented at Ellwood since the 1920s;
  eucalyptus planted by Ellwood Cooper (1870s); Xerces ranks Ellwood 4th-highest-
  priority CA site; western monarchs down >95% from historic size.

## Engineering societies

- **Los Ingenieros** — UCSB SHPE/MAES chapter, founded 1978 by eight Latino
  engineering students (first met in IV apartments); SHPE chapter since early
  1990s; partners with **NSBE** and El Congreso; **Student Organization of the
  Year 2001–02** (also 1998–99, 2008–09, 2010–11).
- **NSBE** — National Society of Black Engineers, founded 1975.

## Downtown and the pass

- **Bill's Bus**: founded 1991 by William J. Singer (19-year-old UCSB student);
  $7 + $1 gas surcharge; Tue/Thu/Fri/Sat, IV from ~8:30 p.m., last SB bus 2 a.m.;
  ~25,000 riders/yr; suspended 2002, sold 2004 to Craig Jenkins (Velvet Jones);
  Embarcadero pickup. LA Times 5/19/2003; LocalWiki IV; SB Independent 4/21/2010.
- **Arlington Theatre** — 1317 State St (1929 Mission Revival palace).
- **The Grad / Embarcadero Hall**: SB Independent 10/16/2008 — Embarcadero Hall
  (rebuilt BofA, burned 1970) → The Graduate → The Anaconda → IV Brewing → IV
  Billiards. The remembered "Grad bar off Hollister w/ graduate student lounge"
  stays memory tier.
- **Cold Spring Tavern** — 5995 Stagecoach Rd, Hwy 154; stagecoach relay from 1868
  (the Wells Fargo-era stop); stages ended 1901.
- **Knapp's Castle** — George Knapp's 1916 lodge, burned 1940; stone arches above
  Lake Cachuma.
- **Lizard's Mouth** — sandstone fin on West Camino Cielo; full-moon gathering
  spot (community knowledge; attendance memory).
- **Live Oak Camp** — county's 40-acre reservation-only group camp on the Santa
  Ynez River, ~5 mi south of the Cachuma entrance on Hwy 154, **sharing its
  entrance road with Rancho San Marcos golf course** — "the Renaissance faire at
  the golf course, across from Lake Cachuma." Leadership retreat + Magaly = memory.
- **Hope Ranch** — the enclave west of town.
