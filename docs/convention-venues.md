# The convention floor — venue research and source check

Research checked: **28 September 2026**.

This note supports the book chapter *The convention floor* and the schematic VRML
exhibit at `convention-centers.html`. It covers the places named in the
subject's recollection — the **Las Vegas Convention Center**, the **Sands Expo**
(now the Venetian Expo), the **Mandalay Bay Convention Center** with the
**Delano** tower and its **Skyfall** lounge, the **Anaheim Convention Center**,
the **Jacob K. Javits Convention Center**, the **Renaissance Las Vegas Hotel**,
and the **Walter E. Washington Convention Center** — and the shows attached to
them: **Black Hat USA**, **DEF CON**, **CES**, the **AVN Adult Entertainment
Expo (AEE)**, **MD&M West**, and **MD&M East**.

> **Revision, 28 September 2026 (second pass).** The subject then identified the
> Las Vegas convention as **Black Hat**, with a "level up" meeting at Skyfall.
> That single correction resolves most of the dating problem below, and §1 and
> the new §1A have been rewritten around it rather than left as first written.

## 0. What the recollection is, and what this note does with it

The prompt for this pass was a personal memory: the Delano's Skyfall lounge, the
Sands Expo hall, "the CES show above the AVN show," the Las Vegas Convention
Center "that year," the Renaissance hotel, MD&M East and West, and the Anaheim
Convention Center.

Nothing in this repository establishes that the subject attended any of these
shows. The **only** in-repo trace of these events is simulated: `apps/Resume.html`
carries an "Auditor's Rolodex & Filofax Registry" whose contacts are filtered by
`MD&M West`, `RSA Conference`, and `CES`. Those records are demonstration data —
the phone numbers are reserved fictional `555` exchanges (`(714) 555-0105`,
`(619) 555-0377`), the audit log announces "Ingested 12 contacts from RSA, MD&M
West, and CES," and the names are invented. **That app is evidence that the shows
were on the author's mind when he built the tool. It is not evidence of
attendance, and it must never be cited as an itinerary.**

So this note does the one thing the record actually supports: it establishes what
each venue *was*, when it was called what, and which shows were in it in which
years — which in turn lets the memory be dated, corrected, and bounded. On the
book's evidence ladder this material sits at "published record" for the venues
and "personal recollection, unverified" for the attendance.

## 1A. Black Hat, Skyfall, and why the Mandalay Bay pairing works

The clarification that the Las Vegas convention was **Black Hat** — not CES —
makes the Skyfall detail fit instead of clash, because Black Hat and Skyfall are
in the *same resort complex*.

- **Black Hat USA has been held at the Mandalay Bay Convention Center every year
  since 2014.** Before that it ran at Caesars Palace from 2002 through 2013 (and
  at Caesars or the Venetian in the earliest Briefings years). [26](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Hat_(conference))
- The Mandalay Bay Convention Center completed a **$70 million expansion across
  2014–16**, taking it to about **2.1 million sq ft** — roughly 861,000 sq ft of
  it exhibition space across the Bayside halls — and making it one of the five
  largest convention facilities in the country. [27](https://www.reviewjournal.com/business/tourism/expanded-mandalay-bay-convention-center-ready-to-welcome-even-more-crowds/) [28](https://www.proglobalevents.com/blog/mandalay-bay-convention-center/)
- The **Delano tower is part of that same complex**, so "Black Hat by day,
  Skyfall in the evening" is a walk across one property, not a trip up the
  Strip. Black Hat's own vendor-party circuit is built around exactly this: a
  rooftop venue at Mandalay Bay is the standard format for a sponsor's
  invite-only evening meeting. [29](https://nocovervegas.com/guides/blackhat-2026)
- Black Hat USA runs in **late July or early August**, not January — 2 – 7 August
  2025, 1 – 6 August 2026. [30](https://www.splunk.com/en_us/blog/learn/blackhat-defcon-conference.html) [31](https://nocovervegas.com/conventions/black-hat-usa)

**The resulting date window.** Black Hat arrived at Mandalay Bay in 2014;
Skyfall opened in **October 2015**, which is *after* Black Hat USA 2015 ended on
6 August. So a Black Hat trip that included an evening at Skyfall is
**2016 or later**, and **not 2020**, when Black Hat USA ran virtually.

**If the Las Vegas Convention Center was in the same week, it was DEF CON.**
DEF CON moved to the **LVCC West Hall for DEF CON 32, 8–11 August 2024**, after
Caesars abruptly cancelled its contract; DEF CON 33 followed at the West Hall on
7–10 August 2025. [32](https://defcon.org/html/links/dc-archives.html) [33](https://news3lv.com/news/local/hacker-conference-def-con-moves-from-caesars-to-las-vegas-convention-center-lvcc-southern-nevada)
Black Hat at Mandalay Bay **and** the LVCC in one week therefore means
**August 2024 or August 2025** — a two-year window, and a testable one.

That is "Hacker Summer Camp": Black Hat USA at Mandalay Bay, BSides Las Vegas,
and DEF CON at the LVCC, back to back in the same August week. [31](https://nocovervegas.com/conventions/black-hat-usa)

**What still does not fit one trip.** The **AVN-below-CES** memory remains a
different decade: that arrangement ended in January 2011 (§1), five years before
the earliest possible Black Hat-plus-Skyfall evening. The book should carry two
Las Vegas threads — a January CES-era trip and an August Black Hat-era trip —
not one composite week.

**"Level up meeting."** Nothing in the public record ties a meeting by that name
to Skyfall, and this pass found no corroborating source. It is recorded as
personal recollection, unverified: a sponsor or partner meeting at a Black Hat
week rooftop venue is entirely ordinary, but ordinary is not the same as
documented. If a calendar invite, a badge, or a photo survives, it would fix the
year inside the 2024–25 window immediately.

## 1. The earlier thread: the memory braids two different decades

Two elements of the recollection cannot belong to the same trip.

**"The CES show above the AVN show," at the Sands Expo.** Until 2012 the AVN
Adult Entertainment Expo was held at the Sands Expo and Convention Center in the
same week as CES. [1](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/AVN_Adult_Entertainment_Expo)
In 2012 AEE moved its dates to the following weekend and moved venues to the Hard
Rock Hotel, and the Las Vegas Sun reported the split at the time, noting that the
show had started "in the basement of the Sands Expo and Convention Center in the
1980s." [2](https://lasvegassun.com/news/2012/jan/17/why-vegas-porn-convention-decided-meet-week-after-/)
The two shows did not share a week again for more than a decade; they only
overlapped again in January 2023, by which time AEE was at Resorts World and CES
was at the LVCC — two different buildings about a mile apart. [3](https://www.fastcompany.com/90732919/ces-aee-weird-tech-trade-show-porn)

The "above/below" detail is not a trick of memory: the Sands Expo genuinely
stacks that way. Its exhibit halls A, B, C, and D are upper-level halls with
roughly 32½-foot ceilings, while **Hall G is the low-ceiling lower hall — about
380,000 sq ft at 13 ft 5 in**, on a slab rated for unlimited floor load. [4](https://grokipedia.com/page/Venetian_Expo)
A CES hall above and an adult expo in the lower hall is a physically accurate
description of that building.

**Therefore: any visit in which CES and AVN were in the same building, in the
same week, happened in January 2011 or earlier.**

**Skyfall at the Delano.** The Delano did not exist under that name until 2014.
The tower opened 17 December 2003 as THEhotel at Mandalay Bay; MGM announced the
Delano rebrand in August 2012, construction began April 2014, and the ribbon
cutting was **2 September 2014**. Mix and Mix Lounge — the previous tenants of
the 64th floor — were replaced by Alain Ducasse's Rivea and **Skyfall Lounge in
October 2015**. The property was renamed **W Las Vegas on 18 December 2024**, and
Skyfall survived the rebrand. [5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W_Las_Vegas) [6](https://www.themeetingmagazines.com/news/ducasse-debuts-rivea-skyfall-lounge-delano-las-vegas/)

**Therefore: any evening at Skyfall happened in October 2015 or later** — at
least four years after the last CES-week AVN show at the Sands. With the Black
Hat clarification in §1A, the Skyfall evening lands in 2016 or later and belongs
to an August trip, while the Sands stacking belongs to a January trip in 2011 or
earlier.

These are two separate Las Vegas trips, at minimum. The book should not print
them as one week. A fair way to write it is: an early-2010s-or-before CES trip
with the Sands halls stacked one above the other, and a mid-2010s-or-later trip
with a drink on the 64th floor. If the intent is a single narrative week, the
only honest framing is a composite, labelled as one.

A second, smaller correction: Skyfall is a **lounge**, not a restaurant, and as
of this pass it is **temporarily closed for renovation** — it shut on 20 July
2026 with a reopening planned for 4 October 2026. [7](https://www.casino.org/vitalvegas/skyfall-lounge-closes-for-renovation-at-w-las-vegas/)
Present-tense description of a visit there should be past tense in the book.

## 2. Las Vegas Convention Center

- **Address** 3150 Paradise Road; operated by the Las Vegas Convention and
  Visitors Authority on a roughly 200-acre campus of about **4.6 million sq ft**
  across the West, North, Central, and South halls. [8](https://www.multivu.com/players/English/8909751-lvcva-las-vegas-convention-center-expansion-informa-market-world-of-concrete/docs/WestHallFactSheet_1623197150059-907052717.pdf)
- **CES has been in Las Vegas since 1978**, and until 1994 ran twice a year —
  Las Vegas in winter, Chicago in summer. The two-story **South Hall was added in
  2002**. [9](https://www.reviewjournal.com/business/conventions/best-convention-center-in-the-world-reopens-after-massive-renovation-3604125/)
- **West Hall** is the $1 billion, **1.4 million sq ft** expansion built on the
  former Landmark and Riviera sites. It holds **600,000 sq ft of exhibition
  space including 328,000 sq ft column-free**, a 14,000 sq ft terrace, and a
  10,000 sq ft Samsung screen in the atrium. Its first show was Informa's World
  of Concrete, with the ribbon cutting on **8 June 2021**. [8](https://www.multivu.com/players/English/8909751-lvcva-las-vegas-convention-center-expansion-informa-market-world-of-concrete/docs/WestHallFactSheet_1623197150059-907052717.pdf) [10](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/1-billion-las-vegas-convention-center-expansion-debuts-with-first-major-convention-post-pandemic-301308548.html)
- Beneath the campus runs the **Convention Center Loop**, the Boring Company's
  0.86-mile twin-tunnel Tesla people mover, which began operating with the West
  Hall opening. [2021 ASCE account](https://www.asce.org/publications-and-news/civil-engineering-source/civil-engineering-magazine/article/2021/01/new-hall-opens-at-las-vegas-convention-center) [10](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/1-billion-las-vegas-convention-center-expansion-debuts-with-first-major-convention-post-pandemic-301308548.html)
- A separate **$600 million renovation of the legacy halls** began in May 2023
  and was completed for CES 2026. [9](https://www.reviewjournal.com/business/conventions/best-convention-center-in-the-world-reopens-after-massive-renovation-3604125/)

**Dating value.** "The Las Vegas Convention Center that year" is datable from
what the memory contains. A West Hall with the ribbon roof and the Loop station
means **2021 or later**. No West Hall, and the campus is the pre-2021 North /
Central / South plan. Which show was in it matters just as much: CES has been
here since 1978, but **DEF CON** only since DEF CON 32 in August 2024 — so an
LVCC memory attached to Black Hat week is 2024 or 2025, while an LVCC memory
attached to a Sands-era AVN show is 2011 or earlier. [32](https://defcon.org/html/links/dc-archives.html)

**Figure discipline.** Published totals differ because they measure different
things — 4.6 million sq ft is the whole campus, while roughly 2.5 million sq ft
is dedicated exhibit space. The book should use the LVCVA fact-sheet figures and
say which quantity it means.

## 3. Sands Expo → Venetian Expo

- Developed by Las Vegas Sands behind the Sands Hotel; groundbreaking 12 November
  1989, **opened 9 November 1990**, with COMDEX as its first show. Expanded in
  2003, renovated for $35 million in 2013. [11](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venetian_Expo)
- The Sands Hotel closed in 1996 and the Venetian opened in its place in 1999;
  the expo hall outlived the hotel it was named for. [11](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venetian_Expo)
- **Renamed The Venetian Expo effective 2 September 2021**, during the sale of
  the Venetian–Palazzo complex to Vici Properties and Apollo Global Management.
  The in-resort meeting space became the Venetian Convention Center at the same
  time. [12](https://www.reviewjournal.com/business/conventions/signs-beginning-to-change-as-sands-adopts-the-venetian-expo-name-2431639/) [13](https://www.reviewjournal.com/business/tourism/sands-expo-changing-name-to-the-venetian-expo-on-sept-2-2400580/)
- **2.25 million sq ft** of total event space; the largest private convention
  facility in the United States. Hall breakdown: A 178,000 · B 189,000 ·
  C 188,000 · D 100,600 sq ft on the upper level at ~32½ ft ceilings, and
  **G 380,000 sq ft at 13 ft 5 in** on the lower level. [4](https://grokipedia.com/page/Venetian_Expo) [13](https://www.reviewjournal.com/business/tourism/sands-expo-changing-name-to-the-venetian-expo-on-sept-2-2400580/)

**Naming discipline.** If the visit predates September 2021 — which the AVN
detail requires — the building is the **Sands Expo**, and calling it the Venetian
Expo in a first-person passage would be an anachronism. The reverse is also true
for any present-day description.

**Caution.** This is the one show in the list where an unverified attendance
claim carries reputational weight for a named living person whose legal record
the book already discusses at length. Chapter XI's editorial standard applies
with full force: unless the subject supplies a badge, receipt, photograph, or
dated post, the book should describe the *venue arrangement* — documented — and
attribute the visit explicitly to his own recollection, or leave it out.

## 5. Anaheim Convention Center and MD&M West

- The ACC opened in **1967**; the domed **Arena**, 28,000 sq ft of flat floor
  seating up to 7,500, is the original mid-century structure on Katella Avenue.
  Seven expansions followed, including the Grand Plaza in January 2013. [15](https://sparkoc.com/venue/anaheim-convention-center/) [16](https://assets.simpleviewinc.com/simpleview/image/upload/v1/clients/anaheimca/ACC_Spec_Guide_2025_v8_final_reduced_77890cd3-63c3-48e9-886d-703bc25dfb33.pdf)
- **ACC North** — the seventh expansion, on what had been car park 1 — opened
  **26 September 2017**: 200,000 sq ft over two levels, 100,000 sq ft of it
  column-free on the upper level, a 10,000 sq ft balcony over Katella facing the
  Disneyland Resort, a climate-controlled pedestrian bridge, and about 1,350 new
  parking spaces. It took the ACC to **1.8 million sq ft** total with over a
  million sq ft of exhibit space — the largest convention center on the West
  Coast. [17](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/anaheim-convention-center-officially-opens-acc-north-building-300526058.html) [18](https://www.visitanaheim.org/articles/post/acc-north-officially-opens/)
- Facility totals: 99 meeting rooms, 352,000 sq ft of meeting space, three
  ballrooms totalling 238,000 sq ft, 53 acres. [16](https://assets.simpleviewinc.com/simpleview/image/upload/v1/clients/anaheimca/ACC_Spec_Guide_2025_v8_final_reduced_77890cd3-63c3-48e9-886d-703bc25dfb33.pdf)
- **MD&M West** is held annually at the ACC. The MD&M portfolio was **founded in
  1985**; the show's 40th edition ran 4–6 February 2025 and its **41st edition in
  February 2026**, drawing exhibitors from over 43 countries with more than 1,800
  exhibiting companies. It returns **9–11 February 2027**. [19](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/md-m-west-returns-focus-200000600.html)

**Naming discipline.** The show has not always been called MD&M West. Informa
Markets Engineering rebranded its advanced-manufacturing events to the MD&M
brand for the 2025 season: **IME West → MD&M West**, **IME East → MD&M East**,
IME South → MD&M South, and ADM Minneapolis → MD&M Midwest. [20](https://www.mddionline.com/manufacturing/informa-markets-engineering-advanced-manufacturing-events-unify-to-md-m-brand)
A visit in, say, 2019 was to a show that was then branded differently, and the
ACC North building the subject would have walked through did not exist before
late September 2017.

## 6. MD&M East and the Javits Center

MD&M East runs at the **Jacob K. Javits Convention Center**, 429 11th Avenue,
New York. It ran **20–22 May 2025** and is scheduled **19–20 May 2027** —
MD&M East and MD&M South alternate as biennial regional expos, so there is no
MD&M East in every calendar year. [20](https://www.mddionline.com/manufacturing/informa-markets-engineering-advanced-manufacturing-events-unify-to-md-m-brand) [21](https://www.mdmeast.com/) [22](https://www.mddionline.com/events/md-m-east)
Under its former IME East branding the show also ran in December — the 2021
edition was 7–9 December at Javits. [23](https://conventioncalendar.com/us/ny/new-york-city/jacob-javits-center/mdm-east-399792)

So "MD&M East and West" is two cities, two coasts, and — since the rebrand —
two different cadences. The book should not imply a single annual pair.

## 7. Renaissance Las Vegas Hotel

3400 Paradise Road, **adjacent to the LVCC** and about a block from the
monorail; **548 rooms** over 15 floors with 30 suites, built 2004, renovated
2017, and marketed as the largest **non-gaming, smoke-free** hotel in the area,
with roughly 20,000–24,000 sq ft of meeting space. [24](https://www.cvent.com/venues/las-vegas/hotel/renaissance-las-vegas-hotel/venue-de267c7f-28aa-454f-8b00-c57040b11c07) [25](https://www.hotelplanner.com/Hotels/283886/Reservations-Renaissance-Las-Vegas-Las-Vegas-3400-Paradise-Rd-89169)

This is a corroborating detail rather than a colourful one: a walk-across-the-
street, no-casino, no-smoking hotel is exactly the choice of someone working the
LVCC floor rather than the Strip, and it is consistent with a CES-week stay in
any year from 2004 onward.

## 8. What the model does and does not claim

`convention-centers.html` renders six schematic exteriors — LVCC, Sands/Venetian
Expo, Anaheim Convention Center, Javits, the Mandalay Bay Convention Center with
the Delano / W tower and its 64th-floor band, and the Walter E. Washington
Convention Center with Sanborn's *Lingua*. They follow the same rules as the Project Y site
exhibit in `docs/project-y-sites.md`:

- Every footprint, height, spacing, street, and placement is **invented**. No
  survey, site plan, floor plan, elevation, or satellite trace was used.
- Three measurements are sourced rather than invented and are called out in each
  model's `WorldInfo`: the **Sands/Venetian hall split** (upper halls A–D at
  ~32½ ft, lower Hall G at 13 ft 5 in), the **Skyfall band at the 64th floor** of
  the tower massing, and **Lingua's 16-foot cylinders** (boxed, since the export
  writes boxes only). Everything around them is interpretive.
- No interiors, booths, exhibitor layouts, egress routes, fire systems, dock
  operations, or security arrangements are modelled. These are **not** venue
  navigation aids and must not be used to plan an event, a route, or a build.
- The models depict buildings, not attendance. Placing a tower next to a hall in
  one scene does not assert that anyone walked between them.

not a verified property of the clocks; `nsa_museum` is context, not a Sanborn
work, and its gallery list predates the museum's 2022 rebuild.

The K4 panel was also brought forward past the sale: the archive sold for
**$962,500 on 20 November 2025**, Sanborn kept about **$770,000**, the buyers
were a crypto-industry group, submissions now cost $1 rather than $50, the
Smithsonian papers are sealed until 2075 — and **the cipher system that produced
K4 has still not been broken**. Finding plaintext in an archive is not a solve.

### 10.3 The "CES For Dummies guide"

Checked separately and recorded in [`for-dummies-source-check.md`](for-dummies-source-check.md):
no such title exists. The convention-floor books are Wiley's sponsored *For
Dummies, [Vendor] Special Edition* booklets; the Internet Archive has no CES
guidebook, only Mattel's 1978 CES planning memos, Commodore's 1984 Winter CES
preparation file and period magazine coverage; and the "pub crawl" is
unverified.

### 10.2 What this means for the six-model exhibit

The Walter E. Washington model shipped as tab 06 of `convention-centers.html`
is *not* the remembered artifact and does not replace the Sanborn viewer. It
stays as an exterior massing study of the building, and now links to the viewer
so the two are not confused; the sculpture itself lives in the viewer, where it
is modelled and sourced. The five Las Vegas and Anaheim models remain a separate
thread from the Sanborn thread, and the book should not braid them.
