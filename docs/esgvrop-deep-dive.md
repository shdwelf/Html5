Working notes for the ESGVROP chapters of *The Greeran Book* (ch. IV outline, ch. VI
summer chapter), the `esgvrop-4dwm.html` viewer, and the bibliography section

## The agency

- **East San Gabriel Valley Regional Occupational Program & Technical Center
  (ESGVROP)** — a public career-technical education agency.
- Founded **1970** (per the agency's own site copy); governed as a **joint powers
  authority (JPA)** of seven unified school districts: **Azusa, Baldwin Park,
  Charter Oak, Covina, Glendora, Walnut, West Covina**.
- Wayback CDX index for esgvrop.org: **266 captures** spanning the 2001–04 site
  era and beyond.
