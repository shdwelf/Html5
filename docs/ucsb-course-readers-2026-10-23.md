## The two catalogues of record

  Textbooks" by term → department → course/section/instructor (Fall 2026
  ordering open at time of writing). This is the bookstore catalog the task
  names; it is per-section and dynamic, with no bulk export found, so rows
  below cite the durable course pages and use the Store as the live check

- **UCSB Library Course Reserves** — the library holds textbooks *and
  course readers* for free short-loan checkout; if a reader exists for a
  course, this is where a student verifies it without buying

## Cross-check against the algorithms book
The stream asked for a cross-check "against the algorithms book" (the
canon being CLRS, *Introduction to Algorithms*). Finding: **in this
sample, UCSB's own algorithms sequence does not assign CLRS** — 130A
assigns Weiss (in C++), 130B assigns Sahni (in C++), and the randomized-
algorithms seminar assigns Mitzenmacher & Upfal. The department's pattern
is language-matched texts per course rather than one canon across the
sequence; CLRS appears as reference culture, not as a required purchase.
Any future "algorithms book" recipe work should therefore key on Weiss
(130A) and Sahni (130B) chapter maps, not CLRS's.
