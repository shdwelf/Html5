# Source check: EAM.Watch and `shdwfear` on Freenode

**Review date: 2026-11-13.** The request named two separate leads: [EAM.Watch](https://eam.watch/) and **Freenode (`freenode.net`)**. This is a bounded public-source check for the literal string `shdwfear`, **not** an exhaustive search of all IRC channels, a live nickname check, or an attribution of a nick to a person. No Freenode channel or date range was specified. The earlier [EFnet `#pub` source map](historical-networks-and-source-leads-2026-11-13.md), [`#2600` review](irc-2600-shdwfear-enocia-source-check-2026-11-13.md), and [EFnet `#nsl` review](irc-nsl-shdwfear-shdwdruid-shdwelf-source-check-2026-11-13.md) are **different network/channel scopes**.

## EAM.Watch: radio records, not IRC

The site's [operator describes setting it up in November 2017](http://www.sniper7kills.org/) to log **HF radio communications** and explicitly says it does **not** capture every broadcast. The [public message listing](https://eam.watch/) and [search guide](https://eam.watch/search) organize items by broadcast time, radio callsign sender/receiver, types such as `ALLSTATIONS` and `RADIOCHECK`, and optional recordings/submitters. These are community-entered records, **not a Freenode IRC channel archive** or verified source of a person's IRC nickname. No encoded radio message is reproduced here or interpreted as an IRC message, cipher key, or evidence of who sent it.

As a limited site-function check, the public [free-text `shdwfear` search](https://eam.watch/api/search?search=shdwfear&paginate=5&page=1) and [`sender:shdwfear` search](https://eam.watch/api/search?search=sender%3Ashdwfear&paginate=5&page=1) each returned `meta.total: 0`; the [control `sender:MAINSAIL` search](https://eam.watch/api/search?search=sender%3AMAINSAIL&paginate=5&page=1) returned nonzero rows. The [site's published 2024 search implementation](https://github.com/sniper7kills/EamWatch/blob/2a2502132b8aa0bbd1dc5fcfdf0143df645d82ea/app/Http/Controllers/Api/MessageController.php#L29-L89) searches public message text or the radio *sender* field with these two forms; current results were checked separately against the live site. **Neither query is a search of IRC nicknames, all submitter display names, or unpublished radio recordings.** No match in these fields says nothing about whether `shdwfear` used Freenode.

**Conclusion:** No original, dated **Freenode** channel log with a spoken line from `shdwfear` was authenticated in these checks. The Freenode viewer's search error, incomplete channel coverage, and indexed-search limits make this an **unresolved historical question**, not evidence the nick never appeared there. A current server or `WHOIS` result, even if obtained, would not establish who held a nick years ago. **No live IRC socket/WHOIS test was attempted**, and neither `freenode.net` as a website nor `irc.freenode.net` as a connection address is itself a complete chat archive.

**Next evidence gate:** a specific **Freenode channel and approximate dates**, or an owner-provided, rights-cleared client/bot log with server/session header, timezone, source owner, and unchanged original-byte hash. Verify the channel and network before interpreting any `NAMES`/`JOIN`/`QUIT` or `PRIVMSG`, and do not equate the same nick on EFnet, SlashNET, 2600net, or Freenode with the same person. Keep any genuinely recovered private conversation and raw owner-held log in **owner-controlled Drive**, not this public repository. None was recovered or uploaded in this review.
