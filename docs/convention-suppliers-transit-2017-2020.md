# Convention suppliers and transit research, 2017–2020

**Reviewed:** 2026-11-04
**Scope:** dated show editions from 2017 through 2020; emphasis on public business/supplier records, venue plans, and routes.
**Status:** research note, not a badge-scan contact list.

## Data boundary and findings at a glance

- A company announcement or exhibitor directory proves only that the company was listed or announced an exhibit. It does **not** show that the user attended, visited the booth, or had a badge scanned. No badge-scan record has been inferred, added, or queried.
- The user chose **no event-organizer API for now**. Use a user-authorized local CSV/JSON export; no API request, credential, or badge data is used by the app or copied into this repository.
- The Local Directory Desk remains browser-local: it makes no organizer/API calls, and its records are not encrypted or synced. It now has optional event, booth, supplier category, and scan-date fields for an authorized local import. Keep any such data in the local collection; do not publish a badge-scan export here.
- **ADA national-meeting note:** the 2017–2020 chronology below records the national meeting venues and formats only; it does not identify a local/regional dental event.
- **Casino venue interpretation:** the user means events/exhibit floors physically hosted at a casino-resort property or its attached convention venue, not casino-industry companies exhibiting somewhere else. Examples below keep those venue facts separate from company leads.

## San Jose McEnery Convention Center ↔ Caltrain / VTA

The McEnery address is **150 W San Carlos St., San Jose**. Caltrain serves San Jose Diridon; VTA light rail has stops named **Diridon** and **Convention Center**, with the latter at the convention center frontage. The connection is Caltrain to Diridon, then VTA light rail toward Convention Center; confirm the correct line, headway, and service calendar for the exact show day.
