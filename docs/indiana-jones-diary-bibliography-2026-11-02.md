# Indiana Jones and Langdon diary bibliography

**Research date:** 2026-11-02
**Purpose:** Topic-specific bibliography for [`indiana-jones-grail-diary-page-gaps-2026-11-02.md`](indiana-jones-grail-diary-page-gaps-2026-11-02.md). Access dates below are 2026-11-02 unless otherwise noted.

## Primary works and catalog records

### [P1] The 1989 film

*Indiana Jones and the Last Crusade.* Directed by Steven Spielberg. Paramount Pictures / Lucasfilm, 1989. **Primary narrative source** for the fictional diary, its intentionally removed map pages, and the characters' account of who holds those pages. Consult the film itself for the scene; online transcripts are not a substitute for the primary work.

### [P2] The 2008 *Lost Journal* book

Rinzler, J. W. *The Lost Journal of Indiana Jones.* New York: Lucas Books/Pocket Books, 2008. The [Gail Borden Public Library catalog record](https://gailborden.aspendiscovery.org/Record/.b14603482) attributes the book to Rinzler, describes it as one unpaged illustrated volume, and reproduces the publisher's in-universe description. This is a **bibliographic record**, not a page-level verification of the reported gap.

The [Internet Archive catalog record](https://archive.org/details/lostjournalofind0000rinz) identifies the 2008 Lucas Books/Pocket Books publication and lists the holding in `internetarchivebooks`, `inlibrary`, and `printdisabled`. Catalog metadata does not grant permission to redistribute the book or its scans.

### [P3] “Professor Langdon diary” candidate

Brown, Dan. *The Da Vinci Code Travel Journal.* Crown Publishing Group (New York), 2006. [Google Books bibliographic record](https://books.google.com/books/about/The_Da_Vinci_Code_Travel_Journal.html?id=UfdigLZioEMC) gives 160 pages and describes it as a journal for readers retracing Robert Langdon's footsteps. This is a **reader companion**, not evidence of a diary kept by Langdon in the novel.

Dan Brown's official pages provide the surrounding distinction: [*The Da Vinci Code* novel page](https://danbrown.com/books/novels/the-da-vinci-code/), [*Live Like Langdon* travel guide](https://danbrown.com/books/resources/live-like-langdon-travel-guide/), and [*The Da Vinci Code* teacher's guide](https://danbrown.com/books/resources/the-da-vinci-code-teachers-guide/). The travel resources are framed as reader-facing guides inspired by Langdon.

## Production-prop and auction records

### [A1] Propstore, 2022, Lot 183

[Indiana Jones' (Harrison Ford) Grail Diary](https://propstore.com/product/indiana-jones-and-the-last-crusade-1989/lot-183-indiana-jones-harrison-ford-grail-diary/), *Indiana Jones and the Last Crusade* (1989), sold in Propstore's 2022 Los Angeles auction. The listing says multiple diaries were made by Keir Lusby Props, identifies this example as sourced from Lusby with a letter of authenticity, and describes pages and inserts. Its page-count wording (“approximately 102 and 12 blank pages”) is ambiguous; cite it as the listing's wording, not as a settled global count.

### [A2] Christie's, 2012, Lot 143

[Indiana Jones and the Last Crusade, 1989](https://www.christies.com/en/lot/lot-5634750), closed 29 November 2012. Christie's describes one production diary as approximately 114 pages, 12 blank, with prop inserts and provenance documentation. It is an object-specific sale record.

### [A3] Propstore, 2021, reference-photo set

[Grail Diary Reference Photo Set](https://propstore.com/product/indiana-jones-and-the-last-crusade-1989/grail-diary-reference-photo-set/), sold 6 July 2021. The catalog describes 228 color reference photographs of the diary, drawings, and annotations, taken and printed by production. The listing establishes that a production reference set was cataloged; it does not state that the 228 photographs form a complete page-by-page scan of one bound prop.

### [A4] Propstore, 2021, separate production-made pages

- [“Venice Library” Grail Diary page](https://propstore.com/product/indiana-jones-and-the-last-crusade-1989/production-made-venice-library-grail-diary-page/).
- [“Knight Crossing Invisible Bridge” Grail Diary page](https://propstore.com/product/indiana-jones-and-the-last-crusade-1989/production-made-knight-crossing-invisible-bridge-grail-diary-page/).
- [“Grail Cup” Grail Diary page](https://propstore.com/product/indiana-jones-and-the-last-crusade-1989/production-made-grail-cup-grail-diary-page/).
- [“Knight's Catacombs” Grail Diary page](https://propstore.com/product/indiana-jones-and-the-last-crusade-1989/production-made-knights-catacombs-grail-diary-page/).
- [Two production-made “Maps” pages](https://propstore.com/product/indiana-jones-and-the-last-crusade-1989/two-production-made-maps-grail-diary-pages/).

These are cataloged as production-made copies/pages. Their descriptions help identify known witnesses but do not establish the complete page order or prove that any one auctioned map is the exact narrative “map with no names.”

### [A5] Published visual archive reference

Vaz, Mark Cotta, and Shinji Hata. *From Star Wars to Indiana Jones: The Best of the Lucasfilm Archives.* Chronicle Books, 1994/1995 editions. [Open Library's bibliographic record](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL1108588M/From_Star_wars_to_Indiana_Jones) lists both editions and 208 pages. Community page indexes cite this broad visual archive book as a partial source for Grail Diary images; it is not a complete diary facsimile.

## Secondary chronology and replica leads — use with explicit labels

### [S1] PopApostle chronology: 1933–35 gap

Clayton Barr, [“Indiana Jones: The Philosopher's Stone”](https://popapostle.com/Indiana-Jones/html/episodes/The-Philosophers-Stone.htm) and [“Indiana Jones: The Temple of Doom”](https://popapostle.com/Indiana-Jones/html/episodes/The-Temple-of-Doom.htm). These chronology pages report a May 1933 entry followed by four torn page edges and 1935 entries, and describe an FSB note about 1934. They are **secondary observations of the published journal**, not publisher-issued transcriptions. Useful for locating a physical copy; not sufficient to reconstruct the missing text.

### [S2] Community reference: *The Lost Journal*

[Indiana Jones Fandom, “The Lost Journal of Indiana Jones”](https://indianajones.fandom.com/wiki/The_Lost_Journal_of_Indiana_Jones). Summarizes the 1933–35 hole as pages torn out and records the book's in-universe framing. Community-maintained; use as a lead, not as a primary catalog or a complete page inventory.

### [S3] Community reference: 2012 collector-set reproduction

[Indiana Jones Fandom, “Grail Diary (prop replica)”](https://indianajones.fandom.com/wiki/Grail_Diary_(prop_replica)). Reports a 144-page reproduction in the 2012 *Complete Adventures* collector's set and describes it as combining material from the movie prop and *The Lost Journal*. This composite claim is **not independently confirmed by the official product record located in this pass**; keep the set insert distinct from the 1989 production props.

### [S4] IndyProps replica-maker statements

- [Fictionalized Grail Diary replica](https://www.indyprops.com/pp-gdiary1.htm): the maker expressly describes a continuous story filling gaps and claims 282 pages/65 unique pages.
- [Hero-prop reproduction](https://www.indyprops.com/pp-gdiary2.htm): the maker describes a reproduction and claims 282 pages/63 unique pages.

These pages are valuable evidence of a maker's reconstruction choices, not independent confirmation of the original prop's page count or contents.

### [S5] Wayback-captured fan prop page

[IndianaJonesProps Grail Diary page, archived 10 June 2009](https://web.archive.org/web/20090610024844/http:/www.indianajonesprops.com/grail_diary.html). Repeats a 282-page/32-blank-page production claim and discusses signature counting. Treat as fan-research/replica context; it conflicts with auction descriptions of particular artifacts and is not a production inventory.

### [S6] Zen Seeker page index

[Official Grail Diary Pages index](https://zenseeker.net/GrailDiary/OfficialGrailDiaryPages.htm). A community-maintained index of reported page sightings and image sources. Useful for leads and provenance trails; not a complete, independently verified page list.

## Source-use rule

For page counts, identify the **specific physical object, edition, source, and counting unit**. For the missing text, distinguish an actual production photograph or publication from a fan/replica reconstruction. Do not publish scans or reproduce missing copyrighted pages; summarize the evidence and cite lawful sources instead.
