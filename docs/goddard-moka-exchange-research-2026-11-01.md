# Goddard–Moka Higashi exchange: historical research status

**Checked:** 2026-11-01

**Status:** Partial. Later sources support a host-then-travel cycle, but one 2011 report describes outbound trips as annual; the cadence is therefore not settled. The 1996–98 dates, itinerary, and adult chaperone list are unverified. The separate ISO backup is blocked because the image is not available in the checkout or the searched Google Drive results.

## Short answer

The relevant program is the Goddard Middle School–Moka Higashi Junior High School exchange in Moka, Tochigi Prefecture. GSCA's indexed About/timeline dates the sister-school relationship to 1990; Goddard and GUSD publications describe the school exchange. A separately indexed GSCA entry says GUSD organizes the program, but that entry's current URL returns 404 in the fetch tool, so the administrative detail is treated cautiously here. A GUSD newsletter and later visit reports support a host-then-travel rhythm, but a March 2011 Patch story describes Goddard outbound trips as annual. If the two-year rhythm carried back unchanged, **1997 inbound / 1998 outbound** is one plausible projection (and 1996 would be an outbound year); if the inbound phase was offset, **1996 inbound / 1997 outbound** is also possible. The conflicting cadence descriptions mean neither pairing can be preferred confidently, and neither is proof of the 1990s schedule.

No primary or contemporaneous public source found in this pass gives the exact 1996–98 itinerary or GUSD chaperone names. Do not treat the later sample itineraries or any user-supplied name lead as confirmation.

## What the sources establish

- GSCA's indexed About/timeline dates the Glendora–Moka city relationship to 1988 and the Goddard–Moka Higashi sister-school relationship to 1990. Its separate 1998 entry is about rose bushes and cherry trees; it is not evidence of a student-trip date. The About page's indexed text was more complete than the content exposed by the fetch tool.
- A separately indexed GSCA Student Exchange entry says that GUSD organizes the middle-school exchange and that it is not directly affiliated with GSCA. The current route returned 404 during this pass; GUSD school and district publications independently establish that GUSD documents the program.
- A GSCA participant testimonial previously surfaced in search indexing as saying the program's second year was 1992. Its current URL returns 404, so treat it as a promising but presently unverified archival lead. It does not identify which school hosted or traveled in 1992.
- A May 2016 GUSD newsletter says that Goddard had participated for more than 25 years, describes the alternating host/travel pattern, and reports that about 20–25 Goddard students and staff were going to Japan that summer.
- Two later Glendora reports show a host-then-travel sequence: Moka students visited Goddard in November 2011, with a Goddard trip to Japan planned for July 2012; Moka students again visited in November 2013, with Goddard students given priority to travel in summer 2014. The May 2016 newsletter documents another outbound trip. These examples fit an odd-year inbound/even-year outbound pattern in those years, but do not establish a rule for every year.
- A March 2011 Patch story describes a different cadence: it says about 40 Goddard students visited Moka “every summer” as part of an annual exchange. That conflicts with a strict two-year interpretation of the other reports. The sources do not explain whether this refers to a different arrangement or imprecise reporting.
- A GSCA News search-index result also describes a Moka visit to Glendora in 2023 and Goddard/Sandburg travel to Moka in 2024. Its current route returned 404 during this pass, so it is a corroborating lead only and is not needed for the 1990s projection.
- The 1997/98 and 1996/97 pairings are therefore hypotheses to test against records, not conclusions. No contemporaneous 1996–98 report or trip approval has been found.

### Later itinerary examples are not the 1990s itinerary

The 2011 and 2013 reports describe weeklong host-family visits, school attendance, and local activities. The 2011 article mentions Disneyland, Knott's Berry Farm, the mountains, bowling, and a Glendora High football game. It also says the visiting Moka delegation had adult chaperones, but does not give their names. These are later examples, not evidence of the user's 1990s itinerary or the GUSD chaperone list.

A 2019 obituary says Goddard teacher Andrew Reinberger began work there in fall 1998 and later chaperoned a Moka trip after several years at the school. This makes him a documented later chaperone, not evidence for a 1996–98 chaperone list.

## Other requested leads

### GUSD / Dr. Patrick Bushman

A 1992 *Los Angeles Times* article places Patrick Bushman among GUSD board members involved in earlier teacher-contract negotiations; the article says he was then a director of the Claremont Dispute Resolution Center. A 2026 meeting summary refers to Dr. Patrick Bushman as a former GUSD superintendent and describes a district room dedicated in his honor. These sources support a substantial GUSD connection, but this pass did **not** establish his exact superintendent dates or any role in the Moka trip.

## Public-records path

GUSD's current Board of Education site links to its agenda/minutes portal and the older BoardDocs archive. No 1990s meeting record or travel packet surfaced through those public entry points in this pass. The district Superintendent's Office is the practical next route for a written request for historical meeting minutes and exchange records.

A focused written request could ask for publicly releasable, non-student-identifying records for 1995–99, including: Goddard–Moka exchange dates; trip approvals and agenda attachments; itineraries; adult employee/chaperone lists; school newsletters and announcements; and related board minutes. It should request redaction of student-identifying information rather than student records.
