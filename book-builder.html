Local-only intake

Research scratchpad

Use this area for source leads, apartment names, people, events, and memory prompts that are not ready for publication. These notes stay in this browser only and are excluded from the viewer, SFX installer, quine download, and webxdc share until you manually move verified text into a chapter.

Local-only DNA helper

DNA match notes

Track autosomal matches privately with optional shared percent, shared cM, side flags, and working relationship notes. Side labels are planning aids only; overlapping ranges can happen across more than one family relationship.

Local-only objects lane

Vehicles / objects / markings

Track private recollections like vehicles, license plates, bumper text, props, clothing, or found objects without sending them into viewer, SFX, project JSON, or webxdc exports.

Local-only story atlas

Private story clusters by location

This browser-only view groups scratchpad notes and object-lane items by place. Use formats like Calico Ghost Town :: trailer electrical fire when a remembered place is not yet part of the public map.

Local-only milestone lane

School / dance / graduation cards

Keep school-era memories like dances, graduations, ceremonies, companions, and date notes here. These cards stay browser-local and can feed the private timeline lane without entering public exports.

Local-only fiction lab

Alias legend / refrigerator magnets

Build a separate fiction-side legend that swaps real names and places, gives the story a nom de plume, and lets you move phrase magnets around when you hit writer's block. This legend stays in this browser unless you intentionally export a fiction-veil SFX.

Local-only cast lane

Private family tree / cast of characters

Build a private cast list or family tree for your working draft. Names stored here never enter exported book files unless you manually rewrite them into a chapter yourself.

Edge labels support parent, child, sibling, spouse, family, friend, mentor, roommate, and neighbor. Write them from the selected person's point of view, for example: child: self, spouse: Alex, parent: Jean.

Use a local text file or GEDCOM export such as Greeran Family Tree.txt to seed the private cast lane. GEDCOM imports can stay tight to direct relatives or expand to the full connected tree. The import runs in your browser and stays local-only.
