UHS88a Reader

The Universal Hint System (Jason Strautman, 1988) answers the question you pick, one hint at a time, so nothing is spoiled early. This reader covers the original UHS88a format, parsed locally in your browser — no file leaves your machine. (91a/96a hunk files are detected and pointed at OpenUHS.) No hint files are bundled; bring your own .uhs, or try the synthetic demo.
