what this proves

Two backends, one contract: js/lens3d-ref.js shadows the module op for op, and tests/10-lens3d-wasm.mjs requires the two to agree bit for bit on the vertices and on the texture. The DOM surface this page touches — getContext, bufferData, texImage2D — is checked against Chromium's own IDL in tests/09-blink-contract.mjs (see docs/blink-webgl-wasm.md). Layers needing sin/acos/pow stay in js/lens-3d.js: re-implementing libm inside wasm would forfeit that exactness.
