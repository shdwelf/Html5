ORGANIC ACTS — THE U.S. TERRITORIAL LADDER (PUERTO RICO TRACK)

1898-12-10
Treaty of Paris art. IX — Spain cedes Puerto Rico; Congress to set civil rights & political status.
1900-04-12
Foraker Act (Organic Act of 1900) — first civil government; no U.S. citizenship.
1901
Downes v. Bidwell — the Insular Cases invent the unincorporated territory class: "belongs to, but is not a part of."
1917-03-02
Jones–Shafroth Act — collective U.S. citizenship, bill of rights, elected legislature.
1950 → 1952-07-25
Public Law 600 ("in the nature of a compact") → Commonwealth constitution. Internal text changes; external class doesn't.
2016–2022
Sánchez Valle · Aurelius · Vaello Madero — Supreme Court reconfirms the unincorporated classification.
Control case: American Samoa — no organic act at all ("unorganized"), proving the classification layer and the self-government layer are independent variables.

BERMUDA — FROM VIRGINIA COLONY TO BOT, CHARTER BY CHARTER

1609-07
Sea Venture (Virginia Company Third Supply) wrecks on Bermuda — castaways build ships, reach Jamestown 1610.
1612-03-12
Virginia Company Third Charter extends Virginia to include Bermuda ("Virgineola" → Somers Isles). The Plough settles St. George's. Bermuda is legally part of the Virginia colony.
1615
Spun off to the Somers Isles Company — corporate reclassification out of Virginia.
1620-08-01
House of Assembly first sits — oldest continuously operating legislature in the Americas.
1684
Company charter annulled → Crown colony.
2002
British Overseas Territories Act — current class: BOT (the oldest).

CLAIM CHECK ✅

"Puerto Rico is a neutral observer to CARICOM"

Confirmed — observer tier: no vote, no treaty obligations, technical-committee engagement only. The ceiling is structural: PR's organic-act classification leaves foreign affairs with Washington.

CLAIM CHECK ✅

"Bermuda is something else"

Confirmed, and specifically: Associate Member since 2 July 2003 (fifth associate), upgraded from observer after the 2002 Jennifer Smith application — and now running a formal campaign for full membership (2016 UK entrustment letter; CARICOM Legal Affairs Committee review; "Together for CARICOM" programme). Would be the first associate ever to climb to full.

DISAMBIGUATION PANEL

TREATY

Geneva Conventions (1949)

Four IHL treaties + Protocols; 196 parties; guardian: ICRC. Not the basis of the South Africa case — but GC IV runs through the same facts.

TREATY

Genocide Convention (1948)

Art. IX sends state-vs-state disputes to the ICJ. This is the treaty under which South Africa sued. Standing: erga omnes partes (Gambia v. Myanmar).

COURT

International Court of Justice

UN principal judicial organ, The Hague, 15 judges, states only + advisory opinions. President: Yuji Iwasawa. Distinct from the ICC (individuals).

NAME CHECK ⚠

"South African Court of Justice"

No court bears this name. Three resolutions: (a) South Africa at the ICJ (intended sense); (b) SA's Constitutional Court / SCA (al-Bashir 2016; ICC-withdrawal case 2017); (c) the AU's African Court of Justice — merged on paper (Malabo Protocol), never in force; the working court is the African Court on Human & Peoples' Rights, Arusha.

SOUTH AFRICA v. ISRAEL — ICJ DOCKET TIMELINE (STATUS 2026-10-06)

2023-12-29
Application filed under Genocide Convention art. IX + provisional-measures request.
2024-01-26
First provisional measures: claims "plausible"; prevent acts under art. II, preserve evidence, enable aid, report. Binding (LaGrand) — but expressly not a merits finding.
2024-03-28 · 05-24
Further measures (May order: Rafah offensive, crossings).
2024-07-19
Parallel advisory opinion: Israel's occupation of Palestinian territory unlawful (UNGA request — separate proceeding).
2024-10-28
South Africa files Memorial — ~750 pages + ~4,000 pages of exhibits.
2025-04-14 · 10-20
Counter-memorial deadline extended twice (Jul 28 2025 → Jan 12 2026 → Mar 12 2026; order of President Iwasawa; SA opposed).
2025-12-23
Belgium intervenes (art. 63) — joining Ireland, Spain, Mexico, Brazil, Colombia, Türkiye et al.
2026-03
Israel files Counter-Memorial. Case in merits-pleading phase; further rounds reported to run 2027–2029; late-2025 ceasefire did not end the case.
Enforcement gap: ICJ judgments route through the UN Security Council (Charter art. 94(2)), where the veto lives — the recurring theme of this whole research program: adjudication and enforcement are separate ladders.
