Two dossiers from the archive floor: kr0me corp, the 1995–98 underground library at members.tripod.com/~retrotech, and Rodger Ramrod, Nonaz Inc.'s 1996 DOS4GW curio. The lab pulls the original bytes out of the Wayback Machine and the Internet Archive, checks every one against the archive's own SHA-1 index, and only then lets Ghidra's WebAssembly decompiler look at them.
