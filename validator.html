02

Word diagnostics

—

Each word must resolve to one of 2,048 indexed English BIP-39 words. The final word also carries checksum bits.

  1. —

    Word-by-word diagnostics will appear here.

03

How it works

  1. 01
    Wordlist lookup

    Every token is checked against the canonical English list.

  2. 02
    Bit reconstruction

    Words become 11-bit indices, then the entropy bytes are rebuilt.

  3. 03
    Checksum compare

    SHA-256 of the entropy is compared with the encoded checksum.

i

Valid does not mean the phrase belongs to a particular wallet. It only means the encoding is well-formed.
