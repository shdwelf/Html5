Meal Card

Meal credits for the corporation, run like Nano/Banano: every card carries its own chain of blocks, transfers are feeless, and accounting is lossless — integer cents, a receive credits exactly its send, and the till re-verifies every hash before honoring anything. The wallet UX takes after ARRR PirateOcean (one balance, send, receive, memo). Nothing leaves this tab: no server, no network, no float near money.

What this is not
