This page is not a re-implementation. It runs four-corners-calc/core/*.c transpiled by calc/tools/c2js.py into js/four-corners-core.js — the same source sdcc builds for the Z80 and TIGCC builds for the 68k models. make -C four-corners-calc test diffs this build against the gcc build for all three devices and requires every framebuffer hash to match.

Geometry is quantised to 1/2000° (~22 m worst case) straight out of four-corners.xdc; relief is the app's own curated-IDW control points reworked in fixed point, mean error 2 m against the bundle's elevAt(). Only two features carry a schematic depth — the Aneth reservoir at −1700 m and McElmo Dome's CO₂ at −2400 m — hung on a log ramp under the terrain line. Not a survey.
