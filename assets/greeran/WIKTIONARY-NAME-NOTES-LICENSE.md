## Changes made

Changes by this project: the app selects the English etymology or proper-noun
material relevant to a given name and paraphrases it into a short note. It does
not reproduce the entries or their illustrative quotations. The paraphrases
preserve uncertainty where the entry is tentative or disputed; the accompanying
warning says these are name-form notes, not facts about any individual. This
notice and the embedded notes are licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Unrelated app
code, family records, and the baseline GEDCOM are not covered by this
attribution notice.
