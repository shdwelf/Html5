## Sample format entries
- **JPEG/JFIF** (1992, active) — lossy DCT, 10:1 compression; JFIF sub-format
- **JPEG 2000** (2000) — wavelet compression, lossless, ROI coding
- **JPEG-LS** (1999) — LOCO-I lossless/near-lossless; medical imaging
- **PNG** (1996) — DEFLATE + pre-filtering (Sub/Up/Average/Paeth); created to replace GIF after the LZW patent controversy
- **WebP** (2010) — VP8/VP8L/VP8X
- **AVIF** (2019) — AV1 keyframes; ~50% smaller than JPEG; 10/12-bit
- **HEIF/HEIC** (2015) — Apple default
- **BMP** (1985, legacy) · **TIFF** (1986) · **TGA** (1984) · **PSD/PSB** (1990; 30,000² px / 4 EB) · **XCF** (1995)
