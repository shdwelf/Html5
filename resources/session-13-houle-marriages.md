# Research Log — Session 13: the Alfred Houle household and two marriages

> **Register:** src-69–src-73 · **Date:** 7 September 2026  
> **Scope:** follow the Session 11 queue for Alfred Houle of 12 George Street,
> Biddeford, and test the uncited `src-45` pairing **Alfred Houle × Eugénie
> Tremblay**.  
> **Method:** public, no-login material reached through the text research proxy:
> Internet Archive OCR and metadata, Find a Grave memorials carrying obituary
> transcriptions and linked family records, the Maine Genealogy transcription of
> the former Maine State Archives marriage index, and the Library of Congress
> Maine vital-records guide.

This is a research log, not a subject-supplied extract. It distinguishes a
candidate household from the verified Greeran/Hewitt line. No person below is
inserted as an ancestor without a record linking that household to Jeanette
Morin or another established member of the line.

## 1. The 1929–1930 directory answers the wife question

The full-text proxy for the **Biddeford and Saco Directory 1929–1930** returns
this contiguous entry on scanned leaf 172:

> `Houle Albert emp PMCo r 73 Water Alfred (Agnes) emp PMCo h 24 Acorn`
> **`Alfred (Cordelia) h 12 George`**
> `Alice emp (Saco) r 20 Oak Annette shoewkr r 12 George`

The address makes the identification unusually strong. The 1920–1921 volume
had `Houle Alfred policeman h 12 George` and `Houle Edna ... bds 12 George`.
Nine years later Alfred is still at **12 George**, now with spouse **Cordelia**;
Annette is also resident there. This is not the predicted widow entry. It is a
same-address household continuity that names the wife.

**Finding:** for the Alfred Houle whom Session 11 identified at 12 George
Street, the directory evidence contradicts the workbench's **Eugénie
Tremblay** and supports **Cordelia**. The separate Alfred (Agnes) at 24 Acorn
also demonstrates why name-only matching is unsafe.

---

## 2. Obituary-derived memorials identify Cordelia and the marriage

Find a Grave memorial **#108246408** identifies Alfred Houle (10 Oct 1872–19
Jul 1948), buried at Saint Joseph's Cemetery, Biddeford. Its transcribed
obituary says he had served fifteen years in the Biddeford Police Department,
matching the 1920–1921 roster. It names his first wife as **Cordelia Chicoine**
and says their **1892 marriage record** gives Lowell, Massachusetts, as his
birthplace (while explicitly warning that no other record confirms that
birthplace). It also records a second marriage:

- **Alfred Houle × Cordelia Chicoine**, 1892 (exact day not established here).
- **Alfred Houle × Georgianna Doyon**, 26 June 1933, Maine.

Memorial **#108246504** identifies **Cordelia Chicoine Houle** (17 Jun 1873–17
Sep 1932), daughter of Antoine Chicoine and Aurelie Morissette, and wife of
Alfred. Its obituary transcription names surviving children Albert, Marcel,
Mrs Romeo Boissonneault and Mrs Maurice Viger. The 1932 death explains why
Alfred could remarry in 1933 without implying divorce.

A third memorial, **#206886041**, transcribes the obituary of their son Dr Paul
Marcel Houle (1918–2002). It independently calls him the youngest of twelve
children of **Alfred and Cordelia Chicone [sic] Houle** and records his own
marriage to **Lois Marie Jackson on 19 September 1944**. This child-to-parent
record is useful corroboration, though it remains an obituary transcription,
not the 1892 certificate image.

**Evidence grade:** the couple and household are strongly corroborated by the
same-address directory plus two obituary-derived memorials. The exact 1892
marriage details should still be checked against the original state return.

## 3. Record custody and index limitations

The **Maine Genealogy** marriage search states that its 987,098 entries cover
1892–1966 and 1977–2009 and derive from the Maine Department of Human Services
database formerly supplied by the Maine State Archives. It warns that corrected
and original index entries may contain errors and should be checked against the
records. The text proxy exposed the search form and collection statement but
could not submit its dynamic query, so this session does **not** claim an index
row that it did not see.

The **Library of Congress Maine Local History & Genealogy guide** supplies the
custody chain: the State Board of Vital Statistics records for 1892–1922 are at
the Maine State Archives; 1922–1955 records are on microfilm there, with a
bride's index from 1892. Therefore the original return for Alfred and Cordelia's
1892 marriage is the next authoritative pull.

## 4. Genealogical verdict

| Question | Before | After 7 Sep 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Was Alfred Houle of 12 George a real Biddeford policeman? | corroborated in 1920–21 directory | **confirmed across directory + obituary transcription** |
| Was his wife Eugénie Tremblay? | uncited `src-45` claim | **contradicted for this household**: 1929–30 directory names Cordelia |
| Who was his wife? | unknown in Session 11 | **Cordelia Chicoine**, m. 1892; she died 1932 |
| Did he remarry? | not recorded | **Georgianna Doyon**, m. 26 Jun 1933 |
| Is this household ancestral to the verified Greeran/Hewitt line? | unverified | **still unverified**; no bridge record was found |

This result narrows rather than fills the open quarter. If `src-45` intended the
12 George Street policeman, its spouse is wrong. If it intended another Alfred
Houle, it must supply an address, age, child, or marriage record that separates
him from both Alfred (Cordelia) at 12 George and Alfred (Agnes) at 24 Acorn.
Until a bridge to Jeanette Morin is documented, none of these Houles should be
promoted into the ancestral tree.
