# Procedural Ensō Generator (custom-seed build)

> **Register:** src-55 · **Original:** https://bashupload.app/ynkc9c.htm
> **Extracted:** 30 August 2026 · **Type:** self-published standalone HTML5 tool

A second build of the Procedural Ensō Generator (src-54), differing in one respect that
matters: the seed is now typeable.

- **Ensogen ID** — the generated identifier for the circle
- **Custom Seed (optional):** — a free-text field, letting any phrase drive the stroke
- **Generate Ensō** · **Random Terrain** · **Download PNG**

The custom-seed field is the hinge between the two halves of this batch. With it, the ensō
stops being only an image and becomes an *encoding*: a word, a phrase, or a mnemonic can be
the thing that determines the circle. The haiku-wallet builds that follow (src-56, src-57,
src-58) take exactly that step — the circle is drawn from the same material as the key.
