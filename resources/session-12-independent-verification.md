## 1. The immediate family — independently confirmed

**Louise Francis Carey Hewitt** (b. 9 April 1923, Pasadena; d. 27 August
2005, Glendora, of Alzheimer's; longtime Duarte resident). Her published
obituary (San Gabriel Valley Tribune, 9–11 Sep 2005) names:

- Husband of 66 years: **Maurice Frederick Hewitt**.
- Five children: Terry Lee Milwood, Charles Frederick Hewitt, Linda Louise
  Eiselein, **Jean Belle Greeran**, and Robert David Hewitt.
- Nine grandchildren, seven great-grandchildren; private service
  1 Sep 2005, Forest Lawn Covina Hills.

The Legacy guest book, signed 30 Oct 2005 by "**Jerry and Jeanie Glendora**,"
reads in part: *"We, Jerry, Steven and Jeanie worked side by side with mom
and our dad… My Dad Fred and Louise our mom have 5 children."* This is an
independent, third-party record placing **Steven** with his parents
Gerald ("Jerry"/"Fred") and Jeanie Hewitt in Glendora, and corroborates the
family's Monrovia/Duarte community work (Foothill Unity Center Heart in Hand
award, 1998).

> **Book effect:** the chain Steven Greeran → Jeanie Belle Hewitt →
> Louise Frances Carey (m. Maurice Frederick Hewitt) no longer rests on the
> resume or the research file; it is now anchored by a published obituary and
> a contemporaneous guest-book entry.

## 2. Maurice Frederick Hewitt and the contested Hewett quarter — **decided**

FamilySearch's public page for **Clifford LeRoy Hewitt** (21 Jul 1880,
**San Francisco** – 23 Nov 1966, Baldwin Park; buried Forest Lawn Covina),
sourced to the 1930 US Census and California county birth/death/marriage
records, states:

- Parents: **Fred Leslie Hewett** (1856–1902) and **Addie Inez Worth**
  (1858–1944) — FS IDs LCZB-PSR / LCFM-W5B.
- He married **Leslie May McFarland** (1891–1979) on 28 August 1904 in
  Marin County, California.
- Children include **Maurice Frederick Hewitt (1914–2007)** — FS ID
  G7M1-4PH — plus Clifford Maxwell Hewett (1911–1987), Frederick Hewitt
  (1913–1976), Stella May Hewitt (1915–1994), James Robert Hewitt Sr.
  (1918–1995), Harry Leslie Hewitt (1920–1999), Melvin Samuel Hewitt
  (1924–2004), and Leland Hewitt (1905–).
- Clifford was a building contractor (1923, San Leandro; Glendale 1930;
  El Monte 1940).

An SSDI-derived index independently lists Maurice F. Hewitt, b. 27 Mar 1913
(record year varies 1913/1914 between sources), d. 28 Feb 2007, Duarte.

> **Verdict on contested slot Q9/Q10:** the workbench's "**William Hewitt
> (~1858, Down/England) × Sarah Jane Taylor**" (src-45) is **refuted**.
> Maurice's father Clifford was born in San Francisco to Fred Leslie Hewett ×
> Addie Inez Worth — exactly the proven identification in the research file
> (src-10). The English/Irish William Hewitt does not appear in the
> documentary line. The file wins this slot on independent evidence.

## 3. The French-Canadian quarter — **not located in the verified line**

The workbench alone names **François Morin × Eugénie Bérubé** and
**Alfred Houle × Eugénie Tremblay** as the Biddeford, Maine couples
(src-45). Independent records do **not** place them in the verified tree:

- Maurice Frederick Hewitt's parents are Clifford LeRoy Hewitt
  (San Francisco-born, of the Hewett/Worth line) and **Leslie May McFarland**
  — an Ulster-Scots/Irish surname, not French-Canadian. No Morin, Bérubé,
  Houle, or Tremblay appears on Maurice's documented side.
- No obituary, Find a Grave, FamilySearch public page, or Maine parish
  record reviewed today connects the Greeran/Hewitt/Carey family to a
  Biddeford French-Canadian couple in the required generation.

The Biddeford Franco-American context is real and well documented (tens of
thousands of Québec migrants, including Morin/Houle families, to Biddeford's
mills; Maine parish registers at St. André/St. Joseph record Morin, Houle,
Bérubé, and Tremblay marriages). But the *specific* claim that one of these
couples is a Greeran great-great-grandparent remains **uncited**.

> **Verdict:** the Morin/Houle couples stay **leads to verify, not facts**.
> The decisive records would be (a) a marriage/baptism entry naming the
> couple's daughter and her California marriage, or (b) the relevant Maine
> vital record (the ~US$10 postal request the research file already
> recommends). The French-Canadian quarter, if real, does **not** enter
> through Maurice Frederick Hewitt's parents.

## 4. The Carey / Revolutionary War line — independently confirmed

**Find a Grave** (memorials maintained from cited vital records) confirms:

- **PVT Francis Gray Carey** — b. 4 Feb 1897, Pasadena; d. 25 Jun 1976,
  Los Angeles Co.; buried Forest Lawn Memorial Park, Covina (Honor, Map 4,
  Lot 508, Space 3). Memorial #94384623. **Parents linked: Luther G. Carey
  and Millie Ruth (Bickford) Carey.** Children linked on the memorial:
  Charles Dick Carey (1927–1969, Resurrection Cemetery, Montebello) and
  Willard Oscar Carey (1937–1952); the memorial is incomplete (it does not
  list Louise).
- **Millie Ruth Bickford Carey** — b. 16 Nov 1868, Illinois; d. 12 Apr
  1937, Los Angeles Co.; buried Mountain View Cemetery, Altadena (Valley
  View, Lot 1863, Grave 11). Memorial #181036078. **Spouse Luther G. Carey,
  m. 18 January 1896, Los Angeles** (cited to California County
  Birth/Marriage/Death Records 1849–1980); mother listed as Jane E. Dickey
  (1853–1924). Son: Francis Gray Carey.
- **Luther G. Carey** — b. 1867, Indiana; d. 1897; Mountain View Cemetery,
  Altadena. Memorial #7945411. **Parents linked: Joel Thornburg Carey
  (1832–1904, #87170349) and Elizabeth Stanley Carey (1832–1927,
  #87170242).** Siblings include Josephine (Carey) Nordyke, Serepta
  "Reppie" Carey, Lyman W. Carey, and George Stanley Carey.

The **Rootsweb Carey database** (Personal Ancestral File; the same database
the research file cites) independently carries the Indiana Quaker
generations: Joel Thornburg Carey (b. 7 May 1832) × Elizabeth Stanley, with
children Viola, Josephine, Serepta, Niley, Lyman, **Luther Gordon Carey
(b. 23 Jul 1867, "m. Millie Dicker" [Bickford])**, Mima, and George Stanley
Carey — son of Benjamin Carey (1806), grandson of Jonathan Carey (1781),
great-grandson of the patriot.

The **official NSDAR Ancestor Database** (queried directly,
services.dar.org) returns the patriot:

> **CARY, SAMUEL — Ancestor #A020262.** Service: PENNSYLVANIA, Rank:
> PRIVATE. Birth: 4-2-1752, Bucks Co., Pennsylvania. Death: 9-6-1823,
> Ross Co., Ohio. Service Source: PA Archives, 5th Series, Vol. 5,
> pp. 369–370. Service Description: Capt. Robert Gibson, Plumstead
> Township, 1780. Spouse: **Rachel Doane**. Children (per accepted
> applications) include John, Thomas, Samuel, Elias, Rachel, Cynthia, and
> **Jonathan** [m. Ruth Bond].

This matches the research file's Samuel Carey (Plumstead Twp PA Militia,
1780–81) and the chain Samuel (1752) → Jonathan (1781) → Benjamin (1806)
→ Joel Thornburg (1832) → Luther Gordon (1867) → Francis Gray (1897).

> **Verdict on contested slots Q13–Q16:** the workbench's "**George Daniel
> Carey (Kildare/Kerry) × Ann Sophia Bailey**" and "**William C. Carey Jr.
> × Sally LeGendre**" (src-45) are **refuted**. The documented line is the
> Indiana Quaker Carey family, anchored by Find a Grave, the Rootsweb PAF
> database, and the DAR patriot record. The file wins these slots on
> independent evidence. (The DAR listing is a patriot record and a

> claim of its own.)

## 5. Daisy Beatrice's Texas parents (slots 28–29) — **still open**

Francis Gray Carey's wife is carried in the research file as Daisy Beatrice
(b. 19 Sep 1904, Texas; d. 20 Jan 1989, Monrovia). Independent location
attempts this session did **not** recover her maiden name or her parents:

- No Find a Grave memorial under Carey, born 1904 / died 1989, California.
- The free California Birth Index (1905–1995) and California Death Index
  (1940–1997) web forms — which carry the **mother's maiden name** /
  **parents** fields that would close the slot directly — were reachable as
  sites but not queryable through the text-only research proxy (JavaScript
  search). Louise (b. 1923), Charles Dick (b. 28 Oct 1927), and Willard
  Oscar (b. 1937) birth-index entries would each print their mother's
  maiden name and are the cheapest next pull.
- Her 20 Jan 1989 California death certificate (names both parents) and the
  Francis × Daisy marriage certificate (~1921–22, Los Angeles Co.) remain
  the decisive records, exactly as the research file states.

> **Verdict:** slots 28–29 remain **strongly evidenced but unproven**. The
> next step is mechanical rather than archival: query the California Birth
> Index for the three Carey children (or the 1950 census household) to read
> Daisy's maiden name, then confirm her Texas parents on the death
> certificate. This slot is winnable with free records; it is simply behind a
> wall this session's tooling could not operate.
