"You can enter an existing BIP39 mnemonic, or generate a new random one. Typing your own
twelve words will probably not work how you expect, since the words require a particular
structure (the last word contains a checksum)."

- **Mnemonic Language:** English, 日本語, Español, 中文(简体), 中文(繁體), Français, Italiano, 한국어, Čeština, Português
- BIP39 Split Mnemonic cards · "Hide all private info" · auto-compute

- **Derivation Path tabs:** BIP32 · BIP44 · BIP49 · BIP84 · BIP141 — purpose/coin/account/change fields; account extended private/public keys; client presets (Bitcoin Core `m/0'/0'` hardened, MultiBit `m/0'/0`, Block Explorers `m/44'/0'/0'` — xpub only)
