## Letter 1 — NARA Missing Air Crew Report 6353 (email)

**To:** archives2reference@nara.gov
**Subject:** Missing Air Crew Report request — MACR 6353, 26 June 1944, B-17 42-31701, Hewitt

> Dear NARA Reference Staff,
>
> I am researching my [grandfather's brother / great-uncle], **T/Sgt
> Sam M. Hewitt, Army Serial Number 19179305**, of the 301st
> Bombardment Group (352nd Bombardment Squadron), 15th Air Force.
>
> He was the radio operator of B-17G **42-31701**, shot down by flak on
> the **26 June 1944** mission to Vienna, Austria; all ten crew became
> prisoners of war (he was held at Stalag Luft IV). The Missing Air
> Crew Report is **MACR 6353** (Record Group 92, Entry A1 2109-B).
>
> Please advise how I may obtain a copy of MACR 6353, including any
> enclosures (crew list, casualty questionnaires, German capture
> records / Dulag Luft reports). I am happy to pay applicable
> reproduction fees.
>
> Requester: [full name, postal address, phone, email]
> Relationship to veteran: [e.g., grand-nephew]
>
> Thank you for your help.

**What it should yield:** every crewman's name, rank, serial number,
position, and **next-of-kin name/address (1944)** — the key to locating
crew families (pilot Norman L. Gourley's first) who may hold the crew
photograph, and the authoritative crew-position record for Sam.
