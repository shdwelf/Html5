| Camp | **Stalag Luft 4 — Gross Tychow (formerly Heydekrug), Pomerania, Prussia** (later moved to Wöbbelin bei Ludwigslust / Usedom bei Swinemünde) |

**Match confidence: high.** Name "Sam M" (Samuel Melvin), California residence,
Air Corps heavy-bomber crewman — and a serial number (19-179-305) immediately
adjacent to brother Maurice's (19-067-302), the two enlistments issued in the
same western-United-States block in early 1942. Spokeo's people-search profile
for "Sam M Hewitt" independently cross-lists "Sam Melvin Hewitt / Melvin S
Hewitt."

### The "Polish concentration camp" — identified and corrected

**Stalag Luft IV** at Gross Tychow, Pomerania, was a German *Luftwaffe*
prisoner-of-war camp for Allied **airmen** — overwhelmingly American
non-commissioned officer aircrew. It opened to Americans on 12–14 May 1944 and
by October 1944 held 7,089 Americans and 866 British. The site is **Tychowo,
Poland** today (Groß Tychow was in the German pre-war province of Pomerania,
ceded to Poland in 1945).

So the family memory that "Sam was in a Polish concentration camp" is
**substantively right about the place and wrong about the category**: Sam was
a POW in a *Stalag* (a Luftwaffe airmen's camp), not a *Konzentrationslager*
(death/labour camp). The camp simply sits in present-day Poland. In early
1945 its prisoners were force-marched west ("the Black March"), which the
card's "(moved to Wöbbelin … / Usedom …)" note records.

## 3. The capture — 26 June 1944, Vienna (Austria)

The POW card's report date (26 June 1944) and capture area (Austria) line up
with the published mission record of the **301st Bombardment Group (Heavy)**,
a B-17 Flying Fortress group of the 15th Air Force flying from Italy. On
26 June 1944 the 301st (with the 463rd and 483rd Groups) attacked the
**Vienna Lobau oil refinery** in Austria; the same day's Group history records
enemy fighters attacking the formation and B-17s lost over the target area.
A heavy-bomber Technical Sergeant shot down on that raid and captured in the
Austria theatre matches Sam M. Hewitt's card exactly.

The 301st Group's best-known targets were the **Ploiești (Ploesti), Romania**
oil refineries — raided in April–August 1944. The family's remembered phrase
"against Poncho/Pancho" most plausibly garbles **"Ploiești"** (or simply the
Vienna/Austria oil campaign), rather than naming an adversary. Presented here
as a lead, not a finding.

> **On the "radio operator" lead, again:** if the surviving family story
> actually describes the *heavy-bomber* brother, then "radio operator" is a
> standard B-17 crew billet (the radio operator / radio-gunner), and it
> attaches to **Sam (Technical Sergeant, heavy bomber crew)** rather than to
> Maurice (whose own card records carpenter, Air Corps). This is the cleanest
> reading of the two records together — but the specific crew position is not
> on either card and needs the individual personnel file to prove.

## 4. What is still open

1. **Maurice's MOS / unit.** Not on the enlistment card. His Official Military
   Personnel File (OMPF) — requested from NARA NPRC (St. Louis) — or the
   Army Air Forces unit rosters would settle "radio operator" and his unit.
2. **Sam's crew position.** Same — his OMPF (NPRC) and the 301st BG's Missing
   Air Crew Report (MACR) for the 26 June 1944 Vienna mission would name the
   crew and each man's billet. MACRs are on Fold3 and at NARA.
3. **Why Sam's enlistment card is absent.** Neither `HEWITT MELVIN` nor
   `HEWITT SAMUEL` in the enlistment file returns a California b. 1924 match —
   his punch card falls in the known gaps (~1.5 M cards) of that series. His
   *POW* card (RG 389) is the more specific record anyway.
