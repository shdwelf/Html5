I
Editorial premise

How to make a balanced book from an uneven record

The public record around Steven Thomas Greeran is uneven. Officially, the strongest and clearest documentation concerns a California conviction later referenced by Virgin Islands authorities, his registration history in the U.S. Virgin Islands, and the 2025 charge for alleged registration noncompliance.12 Separately, a self-published web resume supplies a much broader but less authoritative map of schooling, work, and volunteer activity across Southern California and Santa Barbara County.9

This book model treats those two evidence classes differently. Official records anchor the legal chronology. Resume material fills out the earlier life-and-work trail without pretending to know motives, feelings, or unwritten episodes. The result is not a traditional memoir, but it is a more balanced timeline than a dossier limited only to the 2025 case.9

The point of this edition is not to romanticize or to flatten the record. It is to keep the whole available timeline in view while labeling what is official, what is self-published, and what remains unverified.
II
Southern California trail

Glendora, Duarte, San Dimas, and Pomona in the published record

The earliest biographical trail now located comes from the self-published resume. It lists Glendora High School for 1997–2001, a Citrus Community College summer-school entry in 2001, and a California State Polytechnic Institute Pomona summer-school entry in 1998.9 Those entries establish a Southern California academic geography before the UCSB years.

The same resume ties Greeran to multiple nearby place references: a canned-food-drive project linked to Duarte; health-care volunteer listings associated with San Dimas; and later lodge and fair-related items in Pomona and the broader Inland Empire.9 In other words, the public resume sketches a regional trail rather than a single hometown-only narrative.

III
Campus & coast

UCSB, Santa Barbara, and Goleta

The resume lists University of California, Santa Barbara in CMPSCI / the College of Engineering from 2001 to 2004.9 Around that period it also claims student-programmer work at the Davidson Library's Alexandria Digital Library and a 2005 digitization role related to the library's wax-cylinder project.9

These UCSB-linked entries are important because they broaden the biography beyond school alone. They place Greeran within campus technical work, digital archiving, and library systems, while nearby volunteer listings place him in the Santa Barbara area through the Santa Barbara Food Bank, the Santa Barbara Botanic Garden, and a renaissance-faire role at Live Oak Camp in Goleta.9

The available sources do not verify every specific dorm, apartment, or class schedule requested for this project, so those details are intentionally left out of the published narrative unless the user later supplies direct documentation. The vault on this page is where such material can be kept privately while the sourced edition stays cleanly labeled.

IV
Work, service, and craft

A technical and volunteer record before the 2025 case

Greeran's self-published resume describes a varied early-adult work history: Channel Data Systems as Computer Shop Manager in 2004; Novacoast as a programmer in 2004–2005; university library programming; volunteer work for the Santa Barbara Food Bank and Santa Barbara Botanic Garden; and later civic, religious, and service roles ranging from meditation centers to election work and local fundraising.9

The significance of this material is not that every line can be independently confirmed today, but that it complicates any biography reduced to one chapter. A balanced life file needs to show education, labor, volunteerism, and geography alongside the later legal record, while remaining honest about the fact that most of this broader life trail presently survives as self-description rather than institutional biography.9

  • Technical work: Channel Data Systems, library programming, Novacoast, and other computing-adjacent roles.9
  • Service work: food-bank, botanic-garden, health-care, and city-recreation volunteer listings.9
  • Spiritual / civic line: Dhammakaya and Sunnataram entries, fraternal-lodge visits, elections work, and child-ID volunteering.9
V
Official legal record

Registration, wanted notice, arrest, and pending charge in the Virgin Islands

The official record states that Greeran was convicted in California of child pornography with intent to sell or distribute and later registered in the U.S. Virgin Islands after moving to St. Croix. His initial registration date was October 28, 2022, with the most recent published update dated October 27, 2023.12

VIDOJ says he did not appear for the required annual update on October 28, 2024. A wanted notice followed on February 21, 2025, and he was detained on February 23, 2025 near Midre Cummings Park in Frederiksted, then arrested at the Wilbur H. Francis Command Police Station by the SORNA unit.1278

On February 24, 2025, he appeared before Magistrate Judge Venetia H. Velasquez on a charge under 14 V.I.C. § 1724(b)(1) for Failure to Register as a Sex Offender; bail was set at $5,500 and the public release said he remained in custody when unable to post it.2 This charge is an accusation, not a conviction. No further public disposition had been located for this book as of August 28, 2026.

VI
Gaps & private prompts

What remains outside the published edition

Several details requested for expansion — such as named dorm residents, apartment anecdotes, exact class schedules, specific social stories, and many travel memories — were not verified in the public sources reviewed for this project. Publishing them as fact would blur the line between biography and invention, and in some cases would identify third parties who are not public figures.

For that reason, this app separates public book pages from a private embedded vault. Use the vault to capture reminders, chapter fragments, timelines, and personal prompts while the published book remains sourced. If supporting material is later provided, those details can be promoted from private notes into the visible book with clear labeling.

Recommended workflow

  1. Keep verified life facts in the visible book pages.
  2. Keep unsourced memories in the vault until documented or intentionally labeled personal recollection.
  3. Export the vault JSON periodically as a backup while you work.
