What the source check found

Black Hat and Skyfall are one property. Black Hat USA has run at the Mandalay Bay Convention Center every year since 2014 — at Caesars Palace before that — and the Delano tower is part of the same complex, so a conference day and a rooftop meeting are a walk across one resort. Because Skyfall only opened in October 2015, after Black Hat USA 2015 had ended, such an evening dates to 2016 or later, and not 2020, when Black Hat was virtual. If the same week also took in the Las Vegas Convention Center, that was DEF CON, which moved to the West Hall for DEF CON 32 in August 2024 — narrowing the trip to August 2024 or August 2025.

But the AVN memory is a different decade. The AVN Adult Entertainment Expo shared a week and a building with CES only until January 2011; in 2012 it moved to the following weekend and to the Hard Rock. A January CES-era trip and an August Black Hat-era trip are two threads, at least five years apart, and the book should keep them apart.

The “above/below” detail checks out. The Sands Expo really does stack: halls A–D on the upper level at roughly 32½-foot ceilings, and Hall G — about 380,000 sq ft at 13 ft 5 in — below them.

Names have dates. Sands Expo became the Venetian Expo on 2 September 2021; THEhotel became the Delano in 2014 and W Las Vegas on 18 December 2024; IME West and IME East became MD&M West and MD&M East in the 2025 rebrand. ACC North opened 26 September 2017 and the LVCC West Hall on 8 June 2021 — both are hard before/after markers for dating a visit.

The earlier “convention center VRML” was never lost. This checkout, the connected Google Drive, and shdwelf/Html5-sync-incoming were all searched: the only VRML artifact in the sync repo is kryptos_vrml.html, byte-identical to the copies already here. The convention centre in the existing work is Jim Sanborn’s Lingua at the Walter E. Washington Convention Center, already catalogued in sanborn-codex.html — model 06 above.

No attendance is claimed. Nothing in this repository documents that anyone attended these shows; the only in-repo trace is simulated demonstration data in apps/Resume.html, complete with reserved fictional 555 phone numbers. Full reasoning and citations: docs/convention-venues.md.

These models depict buildings, not attendance, and not events. Placing a tower beside a hall does not assert that anyone walked between them. Every footprint, height, spacing and placement is invented except two sourced figures, noted per model.
