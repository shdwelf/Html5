Dish Pointing — GEO look angles

Azimuth / elevation / slant range to a geostationary satellite, from the S5 layer math in js/dish-pointing.js. Spherical-Earth model: good to well under a degree for GEO alignment; add your local magnetic declination before trusting a compass, and expect refraction to lift the apparent elevation slightly near the horizon. Nothing here phones home — pair it with a satellite-longitude table of your own.
