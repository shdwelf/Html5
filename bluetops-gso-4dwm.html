What this shows

Winning contracts signed by GSA contracting offices (FAS 47Q*, PBS 47PA*, HQ 47H*) extracted from the official FPDS-NG ATOM feed, geocoded from vendor-reported addresses, and draped with USGS 3DEP elevations. Each pip is an awardee; each arc is the transportation leg from vendor hub to place of performance. The 4D axis is time — scrub the timeline to replay the award/modification history.

Contract data: FPDS-NG public ATOM feed (SAM.gov is the successor registry; its Contract Awards API needs a personal API key). Elevations: USGS 3DEP ImageServer identify, sampled 2026-09-11, meters NAVD88, vertical exaggeration ×400. Geocodes: FPDS street address → city centroid.
