Safety and limitations

  • Only metadata and bytes are handled; archives are saved as downloads and are not opened or executed.
  • CDX indexing is not proof that replay bytes remain available. A missing replay is reported rather than synthesized.
  • Recursive link extraction is intentionally not attempted in the browser: it requires a server-side fetcher or a user-provided proxy and could cross trust boundaries.
  • This is a dependency-free HTML5 replacement for Warrick's recovery workflow, not a full drop-in Perl implementation.
