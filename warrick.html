🔍 HTML5 DOM Link Extractor & Frontier Generator

In Warrick's original Perl implementation, link discovery required HTML::LinkExtractor and HTML::TagParser. In this HTML5 port, link extraction is handled natively by the browser's built-in DOMParser and TreeWalker APIs, which parse full DOM trees with modern HTML5 forgiving-grammar rules and zero external dependencies.
