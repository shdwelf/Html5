For those who wish to install an extra 9-pin COM port on computers that only have one 9-pin, and one 25-pin...here is a small procedure that can accomplish this using old computer parts:

Here's a pic of what we're trying to accomplish (2 9-pin COM ports):

To do this, you'll need an extra 9-pin COM port ribbon cable that can be salvaged from any old computer. After finding the piece (or just buying it cheaply at a computer store), you need to open the case and find the com port plugs on the motherboard (they might already have COM1 and COM2 installed. Since COM1 should already be a 9-pin, you'll only want to disconnect COM2 and replace with the new ribbon):

Once you have plugged it in, you can let the other side hang out the back of the computer, or install it on a metal slot bracket if you have one for a cleaner fit:
