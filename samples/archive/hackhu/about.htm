HackHU is a website dedicated to the news of the satellite hacking industry.

In September of 2000, HackHU was brought to life by founder DeeEssEss. What started off as a small website soon grew in popularity due to timely and extensive news updates.

During HackHU's opening week, it received merely 45 unique visitors. Four months later in January of 2001, HackHU saw single days where 97,000 visitors browsed its pages, with an overall average of 60,000 unique visitors per day.

HackHU is run by DeeEssEss, who does web design, content management, news updates, consumer relations, and pretty much everything to do with the site. r0f - who joined up with HackHU as it started to get large - is the security engineer and network administrator. He keeps the site's 3 dedicated servers running in top shape at all times...something that is not easy to do.

HackHU is monitored 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. News updates are usually posted within minutes, though there are rare times when it might take longer.

HackHU has chat forums at HackHUForums.com with well over 20,000 members, and an IRC network at irc.hackhu.com (#hackhu) that has an average of 210 users at any given point. During times of crisis, our IRC channel has seen 768 people (January 21, 2001 [Black Sunday]).

HackHU has connections with many of the top experts in the field. This is why such timely news updates are possible. While we are certain you have heard of these experts, we do not feel it necessary to namedrop. Articles are made possible through these experts hard work and generous nature, and at times they are explicitly mentioned.

HackHU has always championed the notion of free information in this industry. There is not one section of HackHU that is only accessible by paying members. We have no paying members, we share everything with everyone for free. While we do ask kindly that if at all possible you donate to the server bandwidth fund using the PayPal buttons on the main page, it should be noted that contributing is completely up to you. The site is no different for those who decide to help out and those who do not.
