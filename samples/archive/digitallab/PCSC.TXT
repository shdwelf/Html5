Jumper for Makinterface Pro for the GIS SM1.0/2.0 PC/SC driver
and .dll Library files.
==============================================================

+--------------------+
| ooooo I:I:II:::: + |    No external power supply required
| ooooo :I:I::::I: - |
+--------------------+

If you use the small SIM Socket on a 504 ot 505 unit, then just
set the DET jumper to normal, this will simulate that the card
is inserted.
