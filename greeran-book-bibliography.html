Storke Tower & the military science course (added October 2026)

"Tall Tales of UCSB's Storke Tower." Santa Barbara Independent, 28 July 2016. UCSB's 175-foot Storke Tower, dedicated 28 September 1969, rises out of the Storke Student Communications building — home of the Daily Nexus, KCSB 91.9 FM and the La Cumbre yearbook offices — and stands taller than any other structure in southern Santa Barbara County. Clark and Morgan's Brutalist design in cast-in-place, board-formed concrete; the three-arched openings on each side reference Mission Santa Barbara; the 61-bell carillon spells "Let There Be Light" at ten minutes to every hour, with Sunday concerts.
