What this is: Headline Harry and The Great Paper Race (1991, Davidson & Associates). You are a rookie reporter for the US Daily Star chasing stories from 1950–1990 across America against the rival tabloid Diabolical Daily. Point-and-click + typing game.

Provenance: four 720 kB FAT12 floppy images from Internet Archive item HeadlineHarry (SHA-1 verified against the item's documented hashes), contents extracted and packed to roms/headline-harry.jsdos, booted by js-dos 8.4.1 (DOSBox → WebAssembly). No files leave the app; webxdc CSP keeps it fully offline.
