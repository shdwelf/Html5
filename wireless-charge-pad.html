Charge Pad
for a ½" × 2" speaker

A mono Bluetooth speaker 0.5 in (12.70 mm) across and 2 in (50.80 mm) tall, plus the pad that recharges it without contacts. Every number below is computed in this tab from the geometry you set: coil inductance from a filament sum over Grover's elliptic-integral solution, coupling from the same filaments, the resonant link solved exactly at the drive frequency, then the rectifier, the CC/CV charge, the thermal rise and whether the parts physically fit in 50.80 mm. The math lives in js/wireless-charge.js and is checked by node tools/verify_wireless_charge.mjs.

Three designs, one geometry

The three presets answer the same brief three ways. As stated (Qi band) takes the dimensions literally and runs the link in the WPC band — and fails, for a reason the frequency chart below makes obvious. Matched micro pair keeps the body and moves the link to 6.78 MHz, which is what actually works at this size. Qi BPP keeps the standard and enlarges the body.

Coils

Self-inductance is the filament method: every pair of turns is coupled through Grover's closed form for two coaxial circular filaments, and the diagonal uses the round-wire loop formula. Resistance is copper at 40 °C with the skin-effect ratio from the exact Bessel solution, applied per litz strand. The proximity derate multiplies it again for the turn-to-turn proximity effect this model does not solve — set it above 1 and watch the answer move; that is how much of the result is uncertain.

The link, solved at one frequency

Both sides tuned to resonance, then the coupled 2×2 solved exactly. The drive voltage is not a fixed 12 V: the tool solves for the voltage that delivers the power the charger asks for, which is what a real transmitter does by walking its input.

What this page does and does not check

  • Checked here: filament inductance against a brute-force Neumann double integral (they agree to ~1e-13 relative), the link solve against the closed-form optimum-load efficiency, the rectifier chain against the link's own power (they must be equal), CC/CV energy in vs stored + heat, and the generated solids against the analytic volume of the profile they were lathed from. Run node tools/verify_wireless_charge.mjs.
  • Not modelled: ferrite field shaping (one measured multiplier, ferriteFactor), turn-to-turn proximity effect (a derate you set), FOD and ASK communication margin, coil self-resonance, and the driver's acoustics. A shipping product needs a network analyser on the real coils and an anechoic measurement of the real driver.
  • The honest headline: a Ø12 mm receiver cannot meet the WPC receiver-coil requirement (Q ≥ 77) with the resistance a 12 mm winding actually has, and a Qi-band link between coils this small runs at a few percent efficiency with amps of coil current. The design that works at this diameter is a matched pair on a resonant link outside the Qi band — which means no WPC certification and an FCC Part 18 filing instead of riding the Qi ecosystem.
