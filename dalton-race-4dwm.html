What this shows

This pass uses the repo's existing USGS 3DEP-style Dalton terrain model and adds pips so you can point at the canyon geometry you meant.

The orange line is an official Amgen Tour of California corridor near Glendora Mountain Road. The blue line is public/community Lower Monroe / Little Dalton trail context. The magenta line is a local-only memory pointer so the remembered Monroe/Little Dalton connection stays separate from verified race-route evidence.

The house-front bike-staging pip is now pinned to the 1803 scene you selected, while still labeled as a local-only memory layer unless corroborated.

Research anchors

  1. AEG / Amgen Tour of California 2019 route announcement: riders went through the outskirts of Glendora and turned left onto Glendora Mountain Road toward Mt. Baldy.
  2. USGS Water Data station 11086500: LITTLE DALTON C NR GLENDORA CA.
  3. Public/community hike directions place the Lower Monroe Motorway along the Little Dalton stream bottom toward Mystic Canyon.
