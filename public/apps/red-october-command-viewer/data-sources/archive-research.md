**Checked:** 2026-10-13  
**Applies to:** Red October · 4D Command Theater  
**Scope:** bibliographic discovery and source/rights notes; not a data dump

## Jane’s Defence Weekly

Internet Archive Advanced Search returned **zero** records for the exact title `Jane's Defence Weekly`. A broader title query (`title:(Jane AND Defence AND Weekly)`) and a full-text phrase query also returned zero on the check date. These are point-in-time catalog/search results, not proof that no differently titled, unindexed, institution-only, or Wayback material exists.

- [Exact-title Advanced Search](https://archive.org/advancedsearch.php?q=title%3A%22Jane%27s%20Defence%20Weekly%22&fl%5B%5D=identifier&fl%5B%5D=title&fl%5B%5D=year&fl%5B%5D=mediatype&rows=50&page=1&output=json) — zero results in the check above.
- [Wayback capture: “JROC to weigh anchor on new USN warship”](https://web.archive.org/web/20090227172628/http://www.janes.com/extract/jdw94/jdw00821.html) — the archived Jane’s page identifies publication date **17 September 1994** and the *Jane’s Defence Weekly* title. It is a partial excerpt/subscription page about the US Navy’s SC-21 surface-combatant program, not a complete weekly issue and not evidence for a submarine layout or the fictional story route.
- [TRID record: “Soviet ‘Typhoon’ class: Leviathan of the Deep”](https://trid.trb.org/View/427904) — a separate Transportation Research Board bibliographic record cites *Jane’s Defence Weekly*, vol. 5, **1 March 1986**, p. 376 (four pages, four figures, three photographs). The record’s abstract characterizes parts of the Typhoon arrangement as “presumed,” “believed,” or estimated. It is a useful citation trail to a contemporary source, but it is **not** an Internet Archive scan or a verified shipyard plan.
- Internet Archive does hold other Jane’s books and annuals, for example [*Jane’s Military Review* (1986)](https://archive.org/details/janesmilitaryrev0000unse); those records are not Jane’s Defence Weekly issues and are not substituted for JDW.

**Use in this app:** only discovery metadata and links are included. No JDW issue scan or article text is redistributed. The TRID abstract is not used as a basis for exact geometry, compartment positions, or a real submarine track.

## Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists

- [Internet Archive: January–February 1987, vol. 43, issue 1](https://archive.org/details/sim_bulletin-of-the-atomic-scientists_january-february-1987_43_1) — IA catalogs this serial issue; its item metadata marks it `access-restricted-item=true`, so reading the scan may require an eligible account or institutional/library access. The related [1987 volume index](https://archive.org/details/sim_bulletin-of-the-atomic-scientists_1987_43_index) is a distinct five-page index item, not the complete run of issues.
- [Nuclear Notebook archive](https://thebulletin.org/nuclear-notebook/) — the Bulletin describes this FAS-authored expert-estimate series as having been published since 1987. It is a separate analytical series, not raw military telemetry, not Jane’s content, and not a source for fictional story-route coordinates.

**Use in this app:** the archive and series are linked as research context only. No arsenal estimates, article scans, OCR, or issue text are imported. The source does not make the viewer a nuclear order-of-battle database.

## Preservation and interpretation limits

The viewer’s stage anchors are authored for this illustration; they were not recovered from a game binary, publication, naval log, or sensor record. The hull is generic exterior display geometry, not a precise internal arrangement. These bibliography pointers do not change those limits. Any later use of an issue should retain its exact title, date, volume/issue/page, stable catalog identifier, access status, retrieval date, and rights statement, and should quote or reuse content only when its license or permission allows it.
