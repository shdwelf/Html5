ABOUT THIS STUDY

A digital visit—not a site survey.

This independent HTML5 experience assembles publicly documented museum stories, campus art and landscape cues into an interpretive 3D model. Separate field-site studies preserve source context without pretending those places occupy the Headquarters campus. Building massing, exhibit placement, paths and distances are intentionally conceptual and not suitable for navigation.
